# Phonics: scope and sequence (rebuilt 13 Oct 2026)
Systematic synthetic phonics, General American sounds, adapted for Arabic speakers. One unit every other lesson (lessons 1, 3, 5 ... ). Every practice word and every story uses only the sounds taught so far, plus the tricky words taught so far (checked by a script). Blending starts in lesson 1. Tricky words come 1–3 at a time with the sounds, not as separate units.

## What was wrong before
- Practice words and stories used letters not taught yet (Group 1 s,a,t,p had *sun* and *top*; its story had *ran, on, mat*). Group 3 had *kite* (magic e, never taught) and *ox*.
- Blending was a separate unit after five letter groups instead of starting on day one.
- Missing: qu, ff/ll/ss, nk, final blends (hand, jump), magic e, igh, oo, ar, or, ir/ur.
- Sight words were whole units on their own; Level 2 sound notation was not IPA (/ay/, /oh/).
