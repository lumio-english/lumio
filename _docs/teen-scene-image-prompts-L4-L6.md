# Teen Track scene images — Levels 4, 5, 6 (60 prompts)

## What these are for

Every Pre-A / Level 1 / Level 2 lesson has a "scene" slide: one real
illustrated moment showing our characters acting out that lesson's key
language, with the sentence in a caption box and a play button. Levels
4-6 don't have these yet. This file gives one scene per lesson (20 per
level, 60 total). Once the images are in, I'll wire the sentences and
translations into the slide generators.

**Save as:** `assets/vocab-scenes/level4/01.jpg` … `20.jpg`, and the
same for `level5/` and `level6/`. **Format:** JPEG, 16:9, minimum
1467 × 825 px (larger is fine), no transparency.

## The cast (Teen Track) — use these descriptions verbatim

- **Omar** — teen boy, short dark hair, warm brown skin, white thobe with
  orange trim, white-and-orange sneakers. Friendly, confident.
- **Sara** — teen girl, orange hijab, glasses, orange cardigan over a
  white top, long orange skirt, orange-and-white sneakers. Warm,
  studious.
- **Noor** — teen girl, orange-and-teal patterned hijab, teal tunic
  dress with patterned trim over white sleeves, orange-and-white
  sneakers. Cheerful, energetic.
- **Ziad** — teen boy, short tousled brown hair, blue-and-yellow zip
  hoodie over a white t-shirt, dark grey cargo pants, blue-and-white
  sneakers, on-ear headphones around his neck. The gamer.
- **Hamad** — teen boy, white ghutra headscarf with black agal, white
  thobe, plain dark sneakers. Traditional Gulf style. Calm, kind.
- **Lumi** — the mascot: a round yellow chick with a feather tuft, big
  eyes, rosy cheeks, small dark backpack with orange trim. Appears in
  the background or corner of some scenes, never the focus.

## Shared style block — append to EVERY prompt

> Wide 16:9 illustrated scene in the Lumio English Teen Track style: flat
> vector cartoon with thick clean dark outlines, smooth soft shading,
> bright warm colors (orange, teal, yellow accents), expressive but not
> exaggerated faces, characters aged 13-16 with realistic teen
> proportions. Cinematic framing with a clear focal action in the
> center-left or center, a real environment with believable depth, and
> the bottom fifth of the image kept visually calm (soft, low-detail) so
> a caption box can sit over it. Culturally appropriate, modest, and
> positive. NO text, letters, numbers, speech bubbles, logos or
> watermarks anywhere in the image. Very high quality, consistent
> character designs exactly as described.

## LEVEL 4 — Smart Choices

**01 · Does She Even Sleep?** — *Does she practice every day?*
> Sara at a desk late in the evening, lamp on, practicing guitar with
> sheet music open, while Ziad peeks in from the doorway looking amazed
> and checking his watch. Bedroom with a small clock on the wall.

**02 · My Routine, My Rules** — *I finish my homework after dinner.*
> Omar at the family dining table after dinner, plates cleared to one
> side, laptop and notebook open, ticking off a routine checklist on a
> wall planner behind him. Warm kitchen light.

**03 · How Often Do You…?** — *I rarely watch TV.*
> Living room at night: a big TV sits switched off and dusty in the
> corner while Noor sits cross-legged on the rug, absorbed in a thick
> book, a stack of finished books beside her.

**04 · Before and After** — *The deadline is on Friday.*
> Hamad standing in front of a large wall calendar, one finger on
> Friday, which has a bold circle around it, holding a folder of
> printed project pages. School hallway, lockers behind.

**05 · Text Me** — *Send it to me.*
> Ziad and Omar on a school bench, Ziad holding out his phone toward
> Omar who is reaching for his own phone; a paper airplane-style
> "sending" motion implied by their gestures. Playground trees behind.

**06 · That's Mine** — *That jacket is mine.*
> Lost-and-found table at school: Sara holding up an orange cardigan
> with a relieved smile, Noor beside her holding a pair of earbuds,
> a cardboard box of mixed items on the table between them.

**07 · Got Any Snacks?** — *Do we have any snacks?*
> Ziad with the fridge door wide open, peering inside hopefully; Hamad
> behind him holding an empty snack bowl and shrugging. Modern kitchen.

**08 · How Much Is That?** — *Check the price tag.*
> Noor in a clothing shop turning over a price tag on a teal jacket,
> eyebrows raised; Sara beside her counting coins in her palm. Racks of
> clothes behind them.

**09 · Saving Up** — *I save some money every week.*
> Omar at his desk dropping a coin into a bright orange piggy bank, a
> small handwritten savings jar beside it half full, a poster of a
> bicycle on the wall he's saving for.

**10 · Best in Show** — *She is the most talented singer.*
> School talent show: Sara on a small stage singing into a microphone
> under a spotlight, the front row (Omar, Ziad, Hamad, Noor) clapping
> and cheering, a banner-free stage backdrop of warm curtains.

**11 · I'd Like…** — *I would like to order a pizza.*
> The five friends at a casual restaurant table, Hamad politely ordering
> from a smiling waiter, a menu open in his hands, others looking at
> their menus. Bright, friendly diner interior.

**12 · You Should Try This** — *You should try it.*
> Noor handing a skateboard to a hesitant Sara at a small skate park,
> Noor's expression encouraging, Sara half-smiling with a helmet on.
> Ziad in the background giving a thumbs up.

**13 · Group Chat** — *She posts every day.*
> Four friends on a sofa each looking at phones but laughing together,
> screens angled away from the viewer (no visible text), Lumi peeking
> over the sofa back. Cozy living room.

**14 · Team Player** — *We support each other.*
> Basketball court: Omar helping Hamad up after a fall, both smiling,
> Ziad and Noor jogging over to help, team spirit obvious. Gym interior.

**15 · Big Decisions** — *There are two options.*
> Sara at a table with two brochures side by side (one science-themed
> with a microscope drawing, one art-themed with a paintbrush drawing —
> pictures only, no words), chin on hand, thinking hard. Library.

**16 · Under Pressure** — *I always feel nervous before tests.*
> Exam hall: Ziad at a desk before a test, pencil gripped, small
> nervous sweat drop, taking a deep breath; Hamad at the next desk
> giving him a calm, reassuring nod.

**17 · My Future Self** — *My goal is to learn coding.*
> Ziad at a computer, screen showing colorful abstract code-like blocks
> (no readable text), a dream-cloud above showing him older in a
> futuristic workspace. Bedroom desk.

**18 · Around the World** — *Every country has its own culture.*
> The five friends around a large world globe in a classroom, Noor
> pointing at a country, small culture icons floating around the globe
> (a lantern, a kite, a drum, a tea pot). No flags with text.

**19 · Reading Adventure: The Group Project** — *They find a compromise.*
> Omar and Sara shaking hands over a messy project table with two
> different poster drafts, Hamad and Noor smiling in relief, Ziad
> taping the two ideas together into one poster.

**20 · Review + Future Plans Project** — *This is my plan for next year.*
> Noor presenting a colorful vision board (pictures only: a book, a
> bicycle, a trophy, a plane) to the class, the other four seated and
> applauding, Lumi on the teacher's desk.

## LEVEL 5 — Telling My Story

**01 · What I Did This Weekend** — *I played football with my friends.*
> Sunny park: Omar and Hamad mid-football game, Ziad celebrating a goal,
> a picnic blanket with Sara and Noor cheering at the side.

**02 · Yesterday's Adventure** — *We saw a great show.*
> Night-time outside a theatre: the five friends leaving, still buzzing,
> Sara recreating a dance move, glowing marquee lights (no text).

**03 · Did You…?** — *Did you finish your homework?*
> Kitchen doorway: Hamad's mother (warm, kind, in abaya and hijab)
> asking Hamad a question while he freezes mid-step with a controller in
> hand, Ziad on the sofa behind trying not to laugh.

**04 · How It Was** — *The mall was crowded.*
> Busy mall interior packed with shoppers, Noor and Sara squeezed
> together holding shopping bags with wide "wow" faces, escalators and
> storefronts behind (no readable signs).

**05 · Back Then** — *There was a long line.*
> Outside a cinema, a very long queue winding around the corner; Ziad
> at the back checking the time, Omar ahead of him waving.

**06 · Time Travel** — *We went to the beach last summer.*
> Photo-album style scene: Sara holding an open photo album, and above
> it a bright memory bubble of the friends at the beach last summer
> building a sandcastle.

**07 · And Then…** — *First, we packed our bags.*
> Four-panel-feel single image: the five friends in a hallway with
> suitcases, Hamad zipping the last bag, Noor holding a boarding pass
> (blank), everyone ready to leave. Morning light.

**08 · Why It Happened** — *I was late because I missed the bus.*
> Omar running down the pavement, schoolbag flying, as a bus pulls away
> at the stop ahead; a wall clock in a shop window shows he's late.

**09 · How I Felt** — *I was proud of my grade.*
> Sara holding up a graded paper with a big star sticker (no numbers or
> letters), beaming, her father (kind, Gulf dress) giving a thumbs up.

**10 · What Was Happening** — *It was raining all morning.*
> Rainy street seen from a café window: Noor inside with a hot drink
> watching heavy rain, umbrellas hurrying past outside, cozy vs stormy.

**11 · Study Story** — *He passed the test.*
> Classroom: Hamad receiving his test paper back from a smiling teacher,
> a green check mark visible (no numbers), Ziad fist-bumping him.

**12 · The Big Trip** — *We flew to a new city.*
> Airplane window seat: Omar looking out at a city skyline below, Sara
> in the middle seat looking over his shoulder, excitement on both faces.

**13 · What Went Wrong** — *I broke my phone because I dropped it.*
> Ziad staring in dismay at a phone with a cracked screen on the ground,
> Hamad crouching beside him with a sympathetic hand on his shoulder.

**14 · The Party** — *There were so many gifts.*
> Living-room birthday party: a table piled with wrapped gifts, a big
> cake with candles, balloons, the five friends mid-celebration.

**15 · A Day to Remember** — *It was an unforgettable day.*
> Golden-hour rooftop: the five friends sitting in a row on a low wall
> watching a sunset over the city, arms around shoulders, Lumi perched
> at the end.

**16 · While It Happened** — *I was texting while I was walking.*
> Ziad walking down a corridor absorbed in his phone, about to walk
> straight into a doorframe, Sara reaching to stop him, comedic timing.

**17 · Interview a Classmate** — *Tell me about your experience.*
> Noor holding a small microphone toward Omar in a classroom "interview"
> setup, Sara filming with a phone on a tiny tripod, Omar mid-story with
> animated hands.

**18 · Movie Night Recap** — *She fell asleep before the end.*
> Dim living room, movie glow on faces: Sara asleep on the sofa arm
> with a blanket, Noor and Ziad still watching, popcorn bowl tipped over.

**19 · Reading Adventure: The Lost Backpack** — *Finally, we found it.*
> School gym: Hamad pulling his backpack out from under the bleachers,
> holding it up triumphantly, the others cheering behind him.

**20 · Review + My Story Project** — *I will tell you my story.*
> Omar standing at the front of the class telling a story, a hand-drawn
> comic strip (pictures only) pinned on the board behind him, classmates
> listening intently.

> out to feel for rain, Hamad already holding an umbrella and smiling.

**03 · This Weekend I'm…** — *I'm meeting my friend on Friday.*
> Noor at a café entrance waving as Sara arrives across the street, both

**04 · While I Was…** — *My phone rang while I was studying.*
> Omar at his desk mid-study, startled by a phone lighting up and

**18 · Comparing Cultures** — *Our cultures are similar in some ways.*

**19 · Reading Adventure: The School Trip** — *The teacher handled it calmly.*
