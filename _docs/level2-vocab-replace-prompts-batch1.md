**Character rule:** a character brief is included only where the picture genuinely needs a person (marked `CHAR:`); everything else is an object or scene with no character. `CHAR:` items only ever use our core young cast (Omar, Sara, Noor, Lumi). Family adults (parents, grandparents, uncle, aunt, man, woman, men, women) are drawn in modest Gulf dress — men in white thobe and ghutra, women in abaya and hijab — and appear only as objects of the word, never as the focus of a scene without a core child where the sentence needs one. No physical contact between boys and girls; any touch is parent–child or grandparent–child only. Everything modest and Gulf-appropriate: no dating cues, no revealing clothing; *dancing* is shown as children's playful movement to a song, not a party.

**Master style** (append to every prompt): Simple flat vector illustration for a young children's English-learning app (ages 6–9). Bright cheerful colours (orange, teal, yellow accents), thick clean black outlines, soft rounded shapes, one centred subject, ISOLATED ON A FULLY TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND (PNG cutout, no background, no shadow plane, no scenery), no text, letters or numerals, no watermark, square 1:1. Characters are children aged 6–8 with slightly oversized heads, short simple limbs, big expressive eyes and rosy cheeks, using the established young designs exactly — **Omar:** short black hair, warm brown skin, white thobe with orange trim at collar and cuffs, orange sandals. **Sara:** glasses, brown wavy shoulder-length hair, orange cardigan over a white collared top, orange skirt. **Noor:** orange-and-teal patterned hijab, teal-and-orange dress with patterned trim, warm brown skin. **Lumi:** the mascot — a round friendly yellow chick with a feather tuft, big eyes, small orange beak, rosy cheeks, stubby arms and legs, cream belly patch.
