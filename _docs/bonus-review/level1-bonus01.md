## Checked and correct (no change)
- Vowel Elevator order a (open) → e (open-mid) → i (near-close): matches jaw/tongue height of /æ/, /e/~/ɛ/, /ɪ/.
- "Lazy A is a little longer": /æ/ is intrinsically longer than /e/ and /ɪ/ in English.
- Fatha ≈ a, kasra ≈ i (stated as "close to"); 3 short + 3 long Arabic vowels (ا و ي).
- Voiced z m n / voiceless s t p; m lips, s air through teeth, t d tongue tip.
- Every answer key: listening 8/8, odd-one-out (sit, pan, ten, bad), swaps (pen, pin, big, sat, tin, men), exit ticket, build rounds, sort, true/false (incl. the three explanations).
- Every word is CVC and decodable with Level 1 phonics, except the names Lazy/Quick/Smiley and "pit/beg/tan/tin" (decodable, meaning not needed).
- Timing 5+10+7+12+10+10+6 = 60.
