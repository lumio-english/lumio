## Left unchanged on purpose (owner to decide)
- Level 1 Bonus 1 deck/homework `say` values (/a/ /e/ /i/ /m/ ...): these are not displayed transcriptions but audio keys; lib/bonus_deck.py turns "/a/" into assets/audio/phonics-sound-a.mp3. Changing them to /æ/ or /ɪ/ would break the audio. In strict IPA, short a is /æ/ and short i is /ɪ/.
- Level 1 Bonus 2 character name "Round O" kept (it is a mnemonic used in gestures and homework); the prose around it now shows the open, unrounded American o as the main form.
