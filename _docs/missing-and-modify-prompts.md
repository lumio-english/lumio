**Scenes — shared style block (append to every scene prompt):** Wide 16:9 illustrated scene in the Lumio English Teen Track style: flat vector cartoon with thick clean dark outlines, smooth soft shading, bright warm colors (orange, teal, yellow accents), expressive but not exaggerated faces, characters aged 13–16 with realistic teen proportions (cast exactly as described above).
