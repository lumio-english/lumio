Standard production format, same as the Level 0–3 batches. This is the **whole of Level 4**: all 174 vocabulary image files, checked against `assets/vocab/` on 29 Sep.

- **REPLACE** (114) — an image exists; regenerate at the new quality bar.
- **NEW** (41) — no image has ever been generated for the word (these are the "first-time" words from the A2 campaign file, plus `bank.png` and `goal-jar.png` which that file missed).
- **Shared with Level 3 — already sent** (19) — the file is used by both levels and you already have its prompt in the Level 3 documents: `hang-out, schedule, deadline, homework, emoji, choice, opinion, notification, teamwork, captain, cheer, decide, compromise, nervous, confident, proud, goal, skill, disagree`. Not repeated here so nothing is generated twice. (Five other shared files — `hobby, earbuds, jacket, sneakers, recipe` — were never prompted, so they ARE included below.)

Each prompt is written to the meaning the Level 4 lesson teaches (lesson + example sentence shown). Lesson 20 is review and reuses earlier files.

**Character rule:** a character brief is included only where the picture genuinely needs a person (marked `CHAR:`); everything else is an object or scene with no character. `CHAR:` items only ever use the core **Teen Track** cast. No physical contact between boys and girls — mixed scenes keep a clear gap; high-fives, fist-bumps and shoulder-arms are boys-with-boys or girls-with-girls only. Everything modest and Gulf-appropriate: no dating cues, no music-video/party imagery, sports kit with long shorts, no revealing clothing.

**Master style** (append to every prompt): Simple flat vector illustration for a teen English-learning app. Bold clean dark outlines, smooth soft shading, rich but not childish colours (orange, teal, yellow accents), one centred subject, ISOLATED ON A FULLY TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND (PNG cutout, no background, no shadow plane, no scenery), no text, letters or numerals, no watermark, square 1:1. Characters are aged 13–16 with realistic teen proportions and use the established Teen Track designs exactly — **Omar:** short dark hair, warm brown skin, white thobe with orange trim, white-and-orange sneakers. **Sara:** orange hijab, glasses, orange cardigan over a white top, long orange skirt, orange-and-white sneakers. **Noor:** orange-and-teal patterned hijab, teal tunic dress with patterned trim over white sleeves, orange-and-white sneakers. **Ziad:** short tousled brown hair, blue-and-yellow zip hoodie over a white t-shirt, dark grey cargo pants, blue-and-white sneakers, on-ear headphones around his neck. **Hamad:** white ghutra headscarf with black agal, white thobe, plain dark sneakers.
