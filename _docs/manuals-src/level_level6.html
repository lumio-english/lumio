Level 6 · Looking Ahead

The final level of the journey: the future tense, predictions, comparisons, and real debate. Your child finishes here able to plan, predict, compare, and argue a point of view in English.

By the end of this level, your child can:

  • Talk about future plans and predictions ("will", "going to")
  • Describe what was happening while something else occurred
  • Talk about rules, duties, and obligations
  • Make comparisons ("even better", "which is better?")
  • Discuss multitasking and daily habits
  • Join a class debate and defend an opinion
  • Compare cultures and traditions

The Looking Ahead Hub

Open any time, not just during lessons: Vocabulary, Idioms, Writing & Speaking, Grammar (page 9). And after every finished lesson, a new round of this level's bonus game, Crystal Ball, unlocks (page 11).

Every lesson has the same shape: new words with pictures, Arabic and audio → listening and matching practice → a grammar point → a short quiz and spelling → homework, a printable worksheet and flashcards. Lesson 20 is the level's Big Review.
Live classes run 45–90 minutes depending on the lesson: a little shorter at the start of the level, and the full 90 minutes for the Big Review in Lesson 20.

Who they're for, and why

Writing practice: Your child writes three original sentences using that day's real grammar pattern -- for example, "going to" for future plans in Lesson 1. Fittingly for this level's own focus on looking ahead, this is where your child practices actually stating their own real plans and predictions, not just recognizing the future tense on screen.

Flashcards: Every card shows the picture and English word on one side and the Arabic translation on the other. That means either parent can quiz their teen at home, even without speaking English themselves -- a quick flip-through is real, screen-free repetition that keeps vocabulary fresh alongside the grammar work.

Crystal Ball — How It Works

Crystal Ball, this level's bonus game

A fortune-telling theme fitting Level 6's future-focused lessons, covering three kinds of visions: reading a word's meaning, judging a prediction, and seeing what happens next in a real conversation.

How to play

  1. Read the crystal ball: an Arabic word appears inside the glowing ball with three English options -- tap the correct meaning.
  2. Judge the prediction: the ball shows a claimed Arabic-to-English match -- your child taps Correct or Wrong to judge it.
  3. A vision unfolds: the ball "shows" two lines of a real conversation, and your child picks what really happens next from three options. Each correct answer across all three round types builds up a "power" meter that makes the ball glow brighter -- a fully-charged meter by the end means "the future looks bright!"
A new round of the game unlocks after every lesson your child finishes, using that lesson's own words — so it is always practice, never random.
