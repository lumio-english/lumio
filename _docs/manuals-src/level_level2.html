Level 2 · My World

The world widens here: extended family, telling time, daily routines morning and night, and the first steps into pointing things out with this/that/these/those.

By the end of this level, your child can:

  • Greet people and introduce themselves properly
  • Name extended family (aunt, uncle, cousin)
  • Use he/she and possessives (my, your, his, her, our, their)
  • Follow classroom commands and count to 100
  • Name the months and tell the time
  • Describe their daily morning and evening routine
  • Point things out with this/that/these/those
  • Make plurals correctly (s/es)

Writing Practice & Flashcards

A printable page for every single lesson

Beyond the screen, every one of Level 2's 20 lessons comes with its own homework sheet, writing sheet and flashcard set, ready to print.

Lesson 1 homework sheet
Lesson 1 flashcards (print double-sided)

Who they're for, and why

Writing practice: Your child still traces and copies each word by hand, but the second task has changed -- instead of drawing, it's now "Talk About It", an open question like "Can you use 'hello' in your own sentence?" This is a deliberate bridge toward Level 3, where full sentence-writing begins, easing your child from copying words into thinking in them.

Flashcards: Every card shows the picture and English word on one side and the Arabic translation on the other. That means either parent can quiz their child at home, even without speaking English themselves -- a two-minute flip-through before bed is real, screen-free repetition that helps new words genuinely stick.

Twelve Months Calendar — How It Works

Twelve Months Calendar, this level's bonus game

A calendar-book theme built around this level's real phonics focus: Lumi says a specific word from the letter sounds your child has already learned, and each correct answer reveals a new month's page, building a full year of the calendar one page at a time.

How to play

  1. Lumi says a specific word built from phonics sounds your child has already covered in this level, and three labeled pictures appear.
  2. Your child taps the picture whose word matches what they heard, reinforcing the letter sounds behind it, not just the picture. A wrong tap costs nothing -- they simply try another option.
  3. A few rounds also use this lesson's own vocabulary and example sentences, covering words and simple sentences too.
  4. A correct answer reveals the next month on the calendar page; by the end of all six rounds, a good run earns a special "Wonderful!" celebration as the book fills up.
A new round of the game unlocks after every lesson your child finishes, using that lesson's own words — so it is always practice, never random.
