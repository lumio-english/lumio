Level 3 · Everyday Life

LUMIO ENGLISH

Real grammar begins here, and the full character cast joins in: Omar, Sara, Noor, Ziad, and Hamad. Conversations get longer, opinions get shared, and your child starts using English the way friends actually talk to each other.

By the end of this level, your child can:

  • Talk about what friends do together (Present Simple)
  • Describe their room with "there is / there are"
  • Say what they can and can't do
  • Ask and answer questions about routines and skills
  • Talk about ongoing actions ("right now")
  • Follow classroom rules and school conversations
  • Read and understand a short story

Lesson 1 homework sheet
Lesson 1 flashcards (print double-sided)

Who they're for, and why

Writing practice: This is where the task changes for good. Instead of drawing or answering an open question, your child now writes three original sentences using that day's real grammar pattern -- for example, Present Simple in Lesson 1. This is production, not recognition: applying the rule your child just learned, not just picking the right answer on screen.

Flashcards: Every card shows the picture and English word on one side and the Arabic translation on the other. That means either parent can quiz their teen at home, even without speaking English themselves -- a quick flip-through is real, screen-free repetition that keeps vocabulary fresh alongside the grammar work.

Crew Chat — How It Works

LUMIO ENGLISH

Crew Chat, this level's bonus game

A group-chat simulation with the full character cast, mixing four kinds of rounds: translating a word, describing a shared photo, spelling a word letter by letter, and understanding a full sentence.

How to play

  1. Translate rounds: a crew member asks how to say an Arabic word in English -- tap the right reply, or spell it back letter by letter using scrambled tiles.
  2. Caption rounds: a crew member shares a photo and asks what's happening -- your child picks which of the lesson's own real sentences actually describes it.
  3. Meaning rounds: a crew member sends a full English sentence and asks what it means -- your child picks the matching Arabic translation, practicing whole-sentence understanding.
  4. Every playthrough mixes all four round types, and a short answer streak gets a hype message from the crew. Correct answers appear as real sent messages, building an actual conversation as the game goes on.
A new round of the game unlocks after every lesson your child finishes, using that lesson's own words — so it is always practice, never random.
