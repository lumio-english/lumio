Level 4 · Smart Choices

LUMIO ENGLISH

Everyday decision-making in English: routines, money, requests, and advice -- the language your child needs to navigate real choices, not just describe the world around them.

By the end of this level, your child can:

  • Describe routines with frequency words (always, never, how often)
  • Talk about sequence (before and after)
  • Ask politely and talk about possessions
  • Ask about prices and talk about saving money
  • Make polite requests ("I'd like...")
  • Give and receive advice ("you should")
  • Talk about future plans and the wider world

Grammar

Frequency adverbs, possessives, requests with "I'd like", modals like "should" -- one grammar point per lesson with its own Grammar Hub rule page, building toward real decision-making conversation.

Writing Practice & Flashcards

A printable page for every single lesson

Beyond the screen, every one of Level 4's 20 lessons comes with its own homework sheet, writing sheet and flashcard set, ready to print.

Lesson 1 homework sheet
Lesson 1 flashcards (print double-sided)

Who they're for, and why

Writing practice: Your child continues writing three original sentences using that day's real grammar pattern -- for example, Present Simple Questions in Lesson 1. This is production, not recognition: applying the rule just learned, in a sentence your child builds themselves, not one picked from a list.

Flashcards: Every card shows the picture and English word on one side and the Arabic translation on the other. That means either parent can quiz their teen at home, even without speaking English themselves -- a quick flip-through is real, screen-free repetition that keeps vocabulary fresh alongside the grammar work.

Squad Budget — How It Works

LUMIO ENGLISH

Squad Budget, this level's bonus game

A shopping-list budgeting game that also practices real conversation: most rounds have your child "buy" items from an Arabic shopping list, and a couple of rounds ask what comes next in a real chat between friends.

How to play

  1. Shopping rounds: an Arabic shopping list appears with priced market stalls -- your child listens for the word Lumi says, then taps the matching stall to "buy" it. A correct purchase adds it to a running receipt and deducts its price; a wrong tap costs nothing, and by the end the receipt shows everything bought plus coins left over.
  2. Conversation rounds: two lines of a real back-and-forth chat appear, and your child picks what really comes next from three options -- genuine dialogue practice, not just vocabulary.
A new round of the game unlocks after every lesson your child finishes, using that lesson's own words — so it is always practice, never random.
