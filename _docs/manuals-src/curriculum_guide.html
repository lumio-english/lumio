A warm welcome to Lumio families

This guide walks you through exactly what your child will learn, in what order, and why -- across the whole 7-level journey. Lumio English is a complete, structured English course built for Arabic-speaking children and teens, ages 4 to 15.

This manual covers the full curriculum: how the levels are structured, what your child will be able to say and understand at each stage, and how everything connects into one continuous, story-driven journey.

Built on the CEFR framework

Lumio English follows the CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) -- the same internationally recognized standard used by real schools and language certifications worldwide. Every level maps to a real, well-defined proficiency stage, so progress here means something outside of Lumio too.

Lumi will be right there with your child, every step of the way

Every Level, Three Goals

LUMIO ENGLISH

The same three goals, every time

Every Lumio level is designed around the same three goals -- built through age-appropriate topics, real vocabulary, and (from Level 3 onward) real grammar:

  • Speak with real confidence -- not just memorized phrases, but language your child can actually use in the moment.
  • Understand real, connected English -- through stories, dialogues, and audio, not isolated word lists.
  • Build steadily on what came before -- every level assumes and reinforces everything already learned.
Confidence to actually speak up -- that's the whole point

A Spiral, Not a Straight Line

LUMIO ENGLISH

Topics come back, deeper each time

Lumio doesn't teach a topic once and move on. Every big idea -- food, family, daily routines, feelings -- returns across multiple levels, each time with richer vocabulary and more complex grammar. Your child isn't just adding new topics; they're building real depth on the ones that matter most.

This is also why review lessons (the last lesson of every level) don't just repeat old words -- they layer in genuinely new, slightly harder vocabulary on the same familiar themes, so review time is still real learning time.

Same big ideas, revisited with richer words each time

What's in Every Lesson

LUMIO ENGLISH

Every one of the 140 lessons follows the same reliable structure, whether it's Pre-A or Level 6 -- only the content and complexity change. Here's exactly what your child does in a typical lesson:

1

New Words 4-10 per lesson

Each new word or phrase is shown with a real illustration, its Arabic translation, and a spoken example sentence with audio.

2

Listen & Practice

Interactive listening, matching, and pronunciation activities that scale with the vocabulary -- every word gets real practice, not just a glance.

3

Grammar Focus Level 3 and up

From Level 3 onward, each lesson introduces one grammar structure with clear rules, examples, and guided practice sentences.

4

Quiz & Spelling Check

A short, gamified quiz confirms understanding before moving on -- instant feedback, no pressure, unlimited retries.

5

Homework & Practice Sheets

Every lesson has a matching printable worksheet, flashcard set, and writing-practice sheet parents can use at home.

Checkpoint lessons (6, 11, 16 & 20): instead of new vocabulary, your child sees one big, richly detailed picture built entirely from words they've already learned, and describes everything they see out loud -- the best way to see real spoken progress.

LUMIO ENGLISH

A deliberate two-step rhythm for every lesson
