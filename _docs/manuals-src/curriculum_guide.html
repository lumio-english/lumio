Welcome to Lumio

A complete English course for Arabic-speaking children

Lumio English takes a child from their very first English word (age 4) to real conversation, reading and writing (age 15) in seven levels. Every level is taught live by a real teacher on Microsoft Teams, in small groups of up to four students at the same level, and every level is supported by an app with prep, homework, games, stories and the English Hub. Arabic is built in from the start: every word, instruction and grammar rule has its Arabic alongside it.

Built on the CEFR

Each level maps to a stage of the CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages), the international standard used by schools and exams worldwide, so progress at Lumio means something outside Lumio too.

How the curriculum is built

Three goals in every level

  • Speak with real confidence — language your child can use in the moment, not memorised phrases.
  • Understand real, connected English — through dialogues, stories and reading texts, not word lists.
  • Build on what came before — every level assumes and reinforces everything already learned.

A spiral, not a straight line

Big topics — family, food, school, routines, feelings — come back in later levels with richer words and harder grammar. Review lessons mix old words with new ones on the same themes, so review is still real learning.

Two tracks, one journey

Kids track (Pre-A to Level 2, ages 4–10): letters and sounds, a full phonics course, first sentence patterns, songs, movement and picture games, with Lumi, Omar, Sara and Noor.
Teen track (Levels 3–6, ages 10–15): one grammar point per lesson, real dialogues between the Crew (with Ziad and Hamad joining), reading, discussion and debate, in a design made for teenagers.

Made for Arabic speakers

The course knows where Arabic speakers struggle: sounds Arabic doesn't have (p, v), vowels, consonant clusters ("isport"), word order, the verb "to be", and he/she/it -s. Every grammar point has a Common Mistakes slide, and the Bonus Classes compare English with Arabic directly.

The learning cycle

1

Prepare in the app

New words with pictures, Arabic and audio before class, earning stars.

2

Live class on Teams

A real teacher, up to four classmates at the same level and lesson, and a supervisor.

3

Homework

Spelling, Say It, drawing or a talk task, a phonics or grammar step and a quiz, plus printable sheets and flashcards.

4

Grow on the map

A lesson is done after prep + live class + homework; then the next stop, the story and the game unlock.

A kids' class (Pre-A–Level 2)

  1. Warm-up and quick recap
  2. Letters and sounds (Pre-A) or Phonics Time with a decodable story (Levels 1–2)
  3. Grammar Time and Common Mistakes, when a pattern is new
  4. New words with picture, Arabic and voice
  5. Build the Sentences and a dialogue
  6. Reading Lab (Level 2, every third lesson)
  7. Games: Memory Match, Sound & Spot, Mystery Picture, quiz
  8. Today I Learned

A teen class (Levels 3–6)

  1. Hook question
  2. Crew Talk: a recorded dialogue
  3. Grammar Time: pattern, rule, practice, 10 checks, Common Mistakes
  4. New words, then 10 vocabulary checks
  5. Describe It and Build the Sentences
  6. Reading Lab (every third lesson)
  7. Story Chain, Your Turn, Two Truths & a Lie
  8. Challenge, real life, 5-minute discussion, recap
Lessons run 45–90 minutes: shorter at the start of a level and the full 90 minutes for the Big Review in Lesson 20. Bonus Classes run 60 minutes (45 in Pre-A).

Phonics (Levels 1–2)

Systematic synthetic phonics: Level 1 teaches every single letter sound (starting with s, a, t, p so children read real words from lesson one), Level 2 teaches sh/ch/th/ng, blends, magic e, vowel teams and vowels with r. Every practice word and story uses only sounds already taught, and tricky words come one to three at a time.

Reading Lab (Levels 2–6)

Six illustrated texts per level, in Lessons 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 and 18, set in places children know (the majlis, AlUla, Riyadh Season, Eid). The teacher reads the first part, students read the second; studied words glow and show their Arabic, and six questions move from Find it to Think to You.

Pre-A · First Words

Ages 4-6 · CEFR Pre-A1 · 20 lessons + 4 Bonus Classes · 164 words · letters & sounds · game: Lumi's Pocket

By the end, your child can:

  • Say hello and goodbye, and their own name
  • Recognize and say all 26 letters, and the sound each one makes
  • Hear the first sound of a word and find its letter
  • Count from zero to ten
  • Name basic colors
  • Name family members and parts of the body
  • Name common animals, foods, toys, and school things
  • Say simple action words and how they feel

In this level

The very beginning of the journey: first greetings, the alphabet with the sound of every letter, numbers, colors, family, and everyday things -- all through play, songs, movement, and repetition, no reading required.

Level 1 · About Me

Ages 6-8 · CEFR Pre-A1 · 20 lessons + 4 Bonus Classes · 188 words · 8 phonics groups · game: Treehouse Builder

By the end, your child can:

  • Form simple sentences with "this is..." / "I am..."
  • Describe size ("big", "small") and count to 20
  • Say what they like and don't like
  • Name rooms, furniture, clothes, and pets
  • Talk about the weather and days of the week
  • Use prepositions like "in", "on", and "under"
  • Say what they can and can't do
  • Read every single letter sound and blend short words (sat, dog, chick)
  • Read a short story using the sounds they know

In this level

Real sentences begin here: describing things, saying what your child likes and doesn't like, and talking about their home, clothes, and abilities. Reading begins too: a step-by-step phonics course teaches the sound of every letter, so your child can blend sounds and read real words and short stories.

Phonics: Group 1: s, a, t, p · Group 2: i, n, m, d · Group 3: g, o, c, k · Group 4: ck, e, u, r · Group 5: h, b, f, l (ff, ll, ss) · Group 6: j, v, w, x · Group 7: y, z, qu · Review: Sounds That Sound Alike

Level 2 · My World

Ages 8-10 · CEFR Pre-A1 · 20 lessons + 4 Bonus Classes · 183 words · 9 phonics groups · game: Twelve Months Calendar

By the end, your child can:

  • Greet people and introduce themselves properly
  • Name extended family (aunt, uncle, cousin)
  • Use he/she and possessives (my, your, his, her, our, their)
  • Follow classroom commands and count to 100
  • Name the months and tell the time
  • Describe their daily morning and evening routine
  • Point things out with this/that/these/those and make plurals (s/es)
  • Read words with sh, ch, th, blends, magic e, and vowel teams (rain, tree, boat)
  • Read a short text and answer questions about it

In this level

The world widens here: extended family, telling time, daily routines morning and night, and pointing things out with this/that/these/those. Reading grows up too: two-letter sounds, blends, magic e and vowel teams, plus a Reading Lab text every third lesson.

Phonics: Digraphs: sh, ch · Digraphs: th (two sounds), ng, nk · Ending Blends: nd, nt, mp, st, lk, sk · Beginning Blends: bl, cl, fl, br, dr, tr · S-Blends: sp, st, sk, sn, sw, sm · Magic e: a_e, i_e, o_e, u_e · Long Vowel Teams: ai, ee, igh, oa · Vowel + r and oo: ar, or, ir, oo · Review: Read It All

Reading Lab: Omar's Big Family · What Is in Grandpa's Pocket? · Good Morning, Class! · Sara's Birthday Picnic · Hamad's Evening · At the Date Market

Level 3 · Everyday Life

Ages 10-12 · CEFR A1 · 20 lessons + 4 Bonus Classes · 237 words · 14 grammar topics · game: Crew Chat

By the end, your child can:

  • Talk about what friends do together (Present Simple)
  • Describe their room with "there is / there are"
  • Say what they can and can't do
  • Ask and answer Wh- questions about routines and skills
  • Compare things ("faster", "bigger")
  • Talk about what is happening right now
  • Read a short text and answer questions about it

In this level

Real grammar begins here, and the full character cast joins in: Omar, Sara, Noor, Ziad, and Hamad. Conversations get longer, opinions get shared, and your child starts using English the way friends actually talk to each other.

Level 4 · Smart Choices

Ages 11-13 · CEFR A1 · 20 lessons + 4 Bonus Classes · 221 words · 13 grammar topics · game: Squad Budget

By the end, your child can:

  • Ask and answer questions about routines (Do/Does)
  • Say how often they do things (always, never, how often)
  • Talk about time and order (before, after)
  • Talk about what belongs to whom (mine, yours)
  • Ask about prices and talk about saving money
  • Make polite requests ("I'd like...") and give advice ("you should")
  • Talk about future goals and other cultures
  • Read a short text and answer questions about it

In this level

Everyday decision-making in English: routines, money, requests, and advice -- the language your child needs to navigate real choices, not just describe the world around them.

Level 5 · Telling My Story

Ages 12-14 · CEFR A2 · 20 lessons + 4 Bonus Classes · 229 words · 10 grammar topics · game: Story Detective

By the end, your child can:

  • Talk about what happened yesterday or last weekend
  • Ask and answer questions in the past ("Did you...?")
  • Describe how something was and why it happened
  • Tell a story in order (first, then, finally)
  • Describe what was happening at a specific moment
  • Talk about a trip, a party, or a memorable day
  • Read a short story and answer questions about it

In this level

The past tense arrives here: what happened, when, and why. Your child moves from describing the present to genuinely telling stories -- their weekend, a trip, a party, a mistake -- in connected, real narrative.

Level 6 · Looking Ahead

Ages 13-15 · CEFR A2 · 20 lessons + 4 Bonus Classes · 225 words · 7 grammar topics · game: Crystal Ball

By the end, your child can:

  • Talk about future plans and predictions ("going to", "will")
  • Describe what was happening while something else occurred
  • Talk about rules and duties ("have to", "don't have to")
  • Say how people do things (slowly, carefully)
  • Compare things ("more interesting", "the best")
  • Join a class debate and defend an opinion
  • Compare cultures and traditions
  • Read a longer text and answer questions about it

In this level

The final level of the journey: the future tense, predictions, comparisons, and real debate. Your child finishes here able to plan, predict, compare, and argue a point of view in English.

Stories, level test & certificate

Illustrated stories that unlock

Pre-A to Level 3 each have a four-part illustrated story, told in that level's own words and read aloud with each word lighting up. Part 1 opens after Lesson 5, then Lessons 10, 15 and 20.

Pre-A — “Lumi's Magic Map”: 1. The Mysterious Map · 2. The Whispering Forest · 3. The Family Picnic Village · 4. The Treasure Celebration

Level 1 — “The Treehouse Club”: 1. The Big Idea · 2. Building Day · 3. Grand Opening Prep · 4. The Backyard Party

Level 2 — “The Twelve Months Club”: 1. New Friends, New Family · 2. School Days · 3. A Year of Birthdays · 4. The Big Poster Party

Level 3 — “The Crew”: 1. Meet the Crew · 2. Around School · 3. Game Night · 4. The Big Match

Level test

After all 20 lessons and 4 Bonus Classes, your child takes a 30-question test built from the level's own lessons (pictures, Arabic to English, sentences, listening), about 15 minutes. Pass mark 70%; Merit from 80%, Distinction from 90%. It can be retaken, and the best score counts.

Certificate

A certificate with your child's name, level, CEFR stage, test score and band, attendance, the teacher's average grade and a list of what they can now do — ready to print or save as PDF.

Free to start: the placement test (about 8 minutes, listening, reading and writing) and a free trial class, in a group or one-on-one, before any payment.
