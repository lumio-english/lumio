Tense = WHEN. Aspect = HOW we look.
  • Tense: past or present (walked / walk)
  • Aspect: a complete dot (did) or from inside (was doing)
  • 'It was raining.' Did the rain stop? We don't know!
  • 'It rained.' Is it complete? Yes.
