Past Simple — Regular Verbs · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I play football yesterday.
I played football yesterday.
Yesterday = past → add -ed.
yesterday = ماضٍ ← نضيف -ed.
2
She was walked to school.
She walked to school.
Don't add "was" to a past verb.
لا نضيف "was" إلى فعل في الماضي.
3
They studyed all night.
They studied all night.
Consonant + y → -ied.
حرف ساكن + y ← -ied.
