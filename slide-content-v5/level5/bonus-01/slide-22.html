The -ed team is winning
help was once a strong verb, but it joined the -ed team. Almost every NEW verb is regular: texted, googled, emailed. Nobody says 'I taxt my friend yesterday'!
كان help فعلاً قوياً ثم انضم إلى فريق -ed، وكل فعل جديد تقريباً منتظم: texted و googled و emailed.
