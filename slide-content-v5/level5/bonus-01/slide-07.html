The last SOUND of the verb chooses
Linguists call this assimilation: a sound copies its neighbor.
  • No buzz (p, k, f, s, sh, ch) → /t/: walked, stopped
  • Buzz (vowels, b, g, v, z, m, n, l, r) → /d/: played, lived
  • t or d → extra beat /ɪd/: want-ed, need-ed
  • The last SOUND, not the last letter: baked = /beɪkt/
آخر صوت في الفعل يختار: بعد المهموس /t/، وبعد المجهور /d/، وبعد t أو d مقطع إضافي /ɪd/.
