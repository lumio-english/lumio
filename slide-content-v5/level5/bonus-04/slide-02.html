Today I can...
  1. 1
    I can find the content words in a sentence and make them strong.
    أستطيع أن أجد كلمات المحتوى في الجملة وأنطقها بقوة.
  2. 2
    I can say weak forms: was /wəz/, were /wɚ/, to /tə/, and /ən/, of /əv/, and know when they become strong.
    أستطيع أن أنطق الصيغ الضعيفة: was /wəz/ و were /wɚ/ و to /tə/ و and /ən/ و of /əv/، وأعرف متى تصبح قوية.
  3. 3
    I can link words (woke_up, lots_of) and pause in thought groups.
    أستطيع أن أصل الكلمات (woke_up, lots_of) وأتوقف بين مجموعات المعنى.
  4. 4
    I can read a short story dramatically, with stress, pauses and feeling.
    أستطيع أن أقرأ قصة قصيرة بأسلوب درامي، بالنبر والوقفات والإحساس.
Big idea
Spoken English has a beat: the important words (content words) are strong and the small words (was, were, to, and, of, a, the) become short and weak, often with the sound /ə/. Words link together and good storytellers pause in the right places.
