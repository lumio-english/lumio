SENTENCE PATTERNS

Because (giving reasons)
“Can you explain why?”
“What's the reason for that?”
“That's not a good excuse.”
“The cause of the delay was heavy traffic.”
“As a result, we missed the first ten minutes.”
“I was late because I missed the bus.”
“It was raining, so we stayed home.”
“Since it was late, we went home.”
“It was raining; therefore, we stayed inside.”

GRAMMAR

Because (giving reasons)
“I was happy because I saw my friend.”
“She was tired because she ran a lot.”
“We stayed home because it was rainy.”
“Why are you sad? Because I lost my toy.”

DIALOGUE

“Can you explain why you were late again? I want the real reason this time.”
“Honestly, since my alarm didn't go off, I just completely overslept.”
“That's a valid reason, not really an excuse. Did you at least text someone?”
“I did, so at least everyone knew I was on my way and not just ignoring it.”
“Good, because showing up late without warning is way worse, honestly.”
“I know, that's exactly why I made sure to explain myself properly this time.”
