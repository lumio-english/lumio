There was / There were · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
There was many people at the game.
There were many people at the game.
Plural → there were.
الجمع ← there were.
2
There were a storm last night.
There was a storm last night.
One thing → there was.
شيء واحد ← there was.
3
There wasn't no tickets left.
There weren't any tickets left.
Plural negative → weren't any (one negative only).
نفي الجمع ← weren't any (نفي واحد فقط).
