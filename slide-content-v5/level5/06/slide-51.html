Time Expressions (ago, last, yesterday) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I met him before two days.
I met him two days ago.
"Ago" comes AFTER the time.
"ago" تأتي بعد المدة الزمنية.
2
We traveled in the last summer.
We traveled last summer.
No "in the" before last summer.
لا نضع "in the" قبل last summer.
3
She called me yesterday night.
She called me last night.
We say last night, not yesterday night.
نقول last night وليس yesterday night.
