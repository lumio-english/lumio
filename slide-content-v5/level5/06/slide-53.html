Two years ago, Sara's school went to AlUla, and she still talks about it. Last week, she found the photos on her old phone. "Noor, look!" she said. "That was the best trip of my life."

They travelled by bus for a whole night and arrived at sunrise. The first stop was Hegra. The tombs were carved into the rock two thousand years ago by the Nabataeans, the same people who built Petra. The guide said the city was a stop on the old spice road. Sara touched the cold stone and felt very small.

A while ago, Noor forgot the second day, but Sara remembered everything. In the afternoon they visited the old town with its mud houses. In the evening, they sat under the Elephant Rock. It was dark, the stars were bright, and the desert was completely silent.

"Earlier today, I checked the website," Sara said. "There is a new trip in March." Noor smiled. "Two years ago we were kids. Now we can take better photos." They booked their places that night. Some memories deserve a second visit.
