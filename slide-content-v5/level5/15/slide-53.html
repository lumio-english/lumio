Sequencing Words (first, then, next, finally) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
First, I woke up. First, I ate breakfast.
First, I woke up. Then, I ate breakfast.
Use first only once; the next step is then/next.
نستخدم first مرة واحدة فقط، والخطوة التالية then/next.
2
Finally I packed, then I left.
First I packed, then I left.
Finally is for the LAST event.
finally للحدث الأخير.
3
At the end, we went home happy.
In the end, we went home happy.
"In the end" = finally; "at the end" needs "of" (at the end of the day).
"in the end" تعني أخيراً؛ أما "at the end" فتحتاج "of" (at the end of the day).
