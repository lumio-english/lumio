Three word-by-word traps
Use ONE rope: because OR so, not both.
  • ✗ before two days → ✓ two days ago
  • ✗ in last week → ✓ last week
  • ✗ Because I was tired, so I slept.
  • ✓ Because I was tired, I slept. / I was tired, so I slept.
قبل يومين = two days ago، والأسبوع الماضي = last week بلا in، واستخدم أداة ربط واحدة فقط.
