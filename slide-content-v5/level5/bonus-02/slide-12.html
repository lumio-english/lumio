Real people tell stories in parts too
In the 1960s the linguist William Labov recorded people telling true stories. Their stories had similar parts, and most had an 'evaluation': the part that says why it matters. 'It was the scariest moment of my life!'
سجّل اللغوي ويليام لابوف أناساً يحكون قصصاً حقيقية، فوجد فيها أجزاء متشابهة وجزءاً يقول لماذا القصة مهمة.
