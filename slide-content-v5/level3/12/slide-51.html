Reading Lab · The New Kid from Jeddah

There is a new kid in Omar's class. His name is Yazan, and he comes from Jeddah. On the first day he is quiet and a little nervous. He sits at the back and looks at his desk.

At break, Omar walks over. "Hi, I'm Omar. Welcome to Riyadh," he says. Yazan smiles. "I don't know anyone here," he says. "I'm from Al-Balad, the old city. My street has a mosque, a bakery and a hundred cats."

Omar laughs. He introduces Yazan to Hamad and Ziad. The boys ask a lot of questions. "Is the sea close to your house?" "Is the old city really old?" Yazan says yes, some houses are more than a hundred years old, and the windows are made of wood.

By Thursday, Yazan is not a stranger. He belongs to the football team, and he has a seat at the front. "The new kid is cool," Hamad says. Yazan only smiles. He is still a little shy, but he is not nervous now.
