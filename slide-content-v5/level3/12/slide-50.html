Articles: a / an / the · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I saw a elephant at the zoo.
I saw an elephant at the zoo.
Before a vowel sound use an.
قبل صوت متحرك نستخدم an.
2
She is an university student.
She is a university student.
"University" starts with a /y/ sound → a.
كلمة university تبدأ بصوت /y/ ← a.
3
I like the music.
I like music.
No "the" for things in general.
لا نضع "the" عند الحديث عن الأشياء بشكل عام.
