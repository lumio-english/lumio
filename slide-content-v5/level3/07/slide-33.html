TEACHER: EXPLAIN THIS RULE
Possessive Adjectives
صفات الملكية
My, your, his, her, its, our, and their go before a noun to show who it belongs to.
my وyour وhis وher وits وour وtheir تأتي قبل الاسم لتوضح ملكيته.
EXAMPLES
This is my bag.
هذه حقيبتي.
Is that your pencil?
هل ذلك قلمك؟
