Eid is in two weeks, and Noor's family cannot agree. Where should they go? Her father wants the mountains of Abha. Her mother wants the Red Sea. Her brother wants to stay home and play games.

"Why Abha?" Noor asks. "Because it is cool in summer," her father says. "The air is fresh, and the houses have beautiful colors." "And why the sea?" "Because I can swim every morning," her mother says.

Noor has an idea. "Let's vote," she says. "Everyone gets one vote, and we need a reason." Her brother disagrees at first, but a vote is fair. He writes 'home' on his paper and draws a game controller.

The vote is two for Abha, one for the sea and one for home. But Noor's mother is a good negotiator, and she knows how to compromise. "Abha this Eid," she says, "and the sea in winter." That is a compromise, and everyone agrees. Even the brother. He can take his games to the mountains.
