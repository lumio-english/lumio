On Fridays, Sara's house is full. Her cousins come from all over Riyadh, and her neighbors come too. There are twelve kids, three grandmothers and one very tired cat.

The boys play football in the courtyard. Sara's brother is the captain of his team, so he gives everyone a job. His teammates are his cousins, and his classmates are his rivals. Nobody is a stranger here.

Inside, the girls help in the kitchen. Sara's aunt makes kabsa in a pot that is bigger than the cat. Noor, Sara's best friend, is not a cousin, but she comes every week. "She is family now," Sara's mother says.

At three o'clock, everyone sits on the floor around one big plate. The grandmothers eat first. Then the kids eat, and the siblings fight over the last piece of chicken. After lunch, the cousins sleep, the cat sleeps, and the house is quiet again.
