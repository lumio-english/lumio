The four rules of English order
  • 1. S - V - O: doer, then verb, then thing.
  • 2. Adjective first: a black hoodie.
  • 3. Adjectives never change: two black hoodies (not blacks).
  • 4. Owner first: Sara's phone, my charger.
  • Your turn: write the four rules in your own words!
