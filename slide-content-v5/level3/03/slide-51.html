Every Friday, Omar's family sits together in the majlis. His grandfather pours gahwa from a golden dallah. The coffee is hot and a little bitter, so they eat sweet dates with it. Omar thinks the smell is awesome.

His little cousin Fahad does not agree. "Gahwa is not my thing," he says. "It is too bitter!" Everyone laughs, but Grandfather just smiles and pours another cup.

After lunch, Omar and Hamad go to a new café in the mall. They order bubble tea with big black pearls and wide straws. Hamad loves it. "This is epic," he says. Omar is not sure. The pearls are weird, and the drink is very sweet.

At home, Omar tells his grandfather about the café. The old man laughs. "Bubble tea is cool for you," he says, "but gahwa is for everyone. Come, sit. I will teach you how to pour it." Omar does not say no.
