Present Simple Negatives · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
She doesn't likes tea.
She doesn't like tea.
After "doesn't" the verb goes back to the base form.
بعد "doesn't" يعود الفعل إلى صيغته الأساسية.
2
I don't want not to go.
I don't want to go.
One negative is enough: don't + verb.
نفي واحد يكفي: don't + الفعل.
3
They doesn't play on Sunday.
They don't play on Sunday.
"Doesn't" is only for he, she, it. They → don't.
"Doesn't" فقط مع he و she و it. مع They نستخدم don't.
