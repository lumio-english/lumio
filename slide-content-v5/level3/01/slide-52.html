Common Mistakes

Present Simple (I/you/we/they) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I am play football on Fridays.
I play football on Fridays.
Habits use the base verb alone -- no "am" before it.
العادات تُقال بالفعل الأساسي فقط — بدون "am" قبله.
2
We goes to school by bus.
We go to school by bus.
With I, you, we and they the verb has NO -s.
مع I و you و we و they لا نضيف -s إلى الفعل.
3
They likes pizza.
They like pizza.
"They" takes the base form: like, not likes.
"They" يأخذ الفعل الأساسي: like وليس likes.
