Ziad can't swim, and he can't bake a cake. But Ziad can do something his friends can't: he can code. He has a small app that tells you when the next prayer is. He made it himself, and his mother uses it every day.

This summer, Riyadh has a huge gaming festival. Thousands of fans come to watch the best teams in the world. Ziad wants to go, but not as a fan. He wants to perform on the stage one day.
