PAIRS / GROUP
Build one story together. Tap Next word — whoever's turn it is adds ONE sentence with that word. Keep the story going round the group; it must make sense!
1-ON-1 WITH YOUR TEACHER
You and your teacher take turns. Teacher starts with the first word; you add the next sentence. Three times round = a whole story. Then retell it alone from the pictures.
