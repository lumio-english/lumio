Present Simple (he/she/it + -s) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
She play tennis every week.
She plays tennis every week.
He, she and it need -s on the verb.
مع he و she و it نضيف -s إلى الفعل.
2
My brother watch TV at night.
My brother watches TV at night.
Verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -s, -x add -es.
الأفعال المنتهية بـ -ch و -sh و -s و -x تأخذ -es.
3
He has go to work.
He goes to work.
Only one verb changes: goes. Don't add "has".
فعل واحد فقط يتغير: goes. لا نضيف "has".
