    I can fix sentences with a missing verb (He teacher -> He is a teacher).

Big idea
Every word in a sentence has a job (noun, verb, adjective, pronoun, preposition), and every English sentence needs a verb, even when Arabic does not: He is a teacher, not He teacher.
