SENTENCE PATTERNS

In the evening, I...
“I come home at four.”
“I do my homework after school.”
“I take a shower at night.”
“We have dinner together.”
“I read a book before bed.”
“I go to bed at nine.”
“My homework is on the table.”
“I put on my pajamas at night.”
“I come home. I do my homework.”
“I take a shower. I have dinner.”
“I read a book in my pajamas. I go to bed.”

DIALOGUE

“What do you do in the evening?”
“I come home, then I do my homework.”
“Do you read before bed?”
“Yes, I read a book, then I go to bed.”
