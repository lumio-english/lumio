Big idea
English words are made of beats called syllables, and one beat is the boss: louder, longer and higher (BREAK-fast, ba-LOON). The plural -s also has rules: it hisses /s/ (books), buzzes /z/ (pens) or adds a whole beat /ɪz/ (boxes).
