Case 4: the heartbeat of English

Clap the word. Put your hand under your chin: your chin drops once for each syllable.
  • Each syllable has one vowel SOUND, not one vowel letter.
  • cake = 1 beat (the e is silent)
  • tree = 1 beat (ee is one sound)
