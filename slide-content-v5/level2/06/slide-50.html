SENTENCE PATTERNS

I have got.../ Have you got...?
“I have got a phone.”
“I have got a new watch.”
“My dad has got glasses.”
“My mom has got a wallet.”
“I have got a key.”
“I have got an umbrella.”
“I have got a camera.”
“I have got a phone. I have got a watch.”
“I have got glasses. I have got a wallet.”
“I have got a key, an umbrella, and a camera.”

GRAMMAR

Have got (I have...)
“I have got a watch.”
“I have got glasses.”
“Have you got a key?”
“Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.”

DIALOGUE

“Have you got a phone?”
“Yes, I have got a new phone. Have you got a watch?”
