The pattern
Have got (I have...)
أملك / عندي (have got)
We use "have got" to say what belongs to us: I have got a phone. To ask, we say: Have you got...? Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
نستخدم have got لنقول ماذا نملك: I have got a phone (عندي هاتف). وللسؤال نقول: Have you got...? (هل عندك...؟) والإجابة: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
