Sara's birthday is in March. This year, her family has a picnic in the park. It is a sunny day, and the grass is green.

Her mother brings a big cake. It has got seven candles. Her aunt brings orange and blue balloons. Her cousins and her friend Noor are the guests.

Sara opens her presents. She has got a new book, a pink watch and a box of colored pencils. "Thank you!" she says to everyone.

Then they sing, and Sara blows out the candles. One, two, three... all seven! Everyone claps. "When is your birthday, Noor?" Sara asks. "It's in May," says Noor. "We can have a picnic too!"
