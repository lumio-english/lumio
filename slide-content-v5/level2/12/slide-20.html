When is your birthday? It's in... · Find the mistake, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
My birthday is on May.
My birthday is in May.
Months take "in".
مع الشهور نستخدم in.
2
When your birthday?
When is your birthday?
The question needs "is".
السؤال يحتاج is.
3
It's in may.
It's in May.
Months start with a capital letter.
أسماء الشهور تبدأ بحرف كبير.
