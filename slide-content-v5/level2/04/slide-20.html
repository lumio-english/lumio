He is / She is · Find the mistake, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
He is my sister.
She is my sister.
A girl -> she.
البنت ← she.
2
She my brother.
He is my brother.
A boy -> he, and we need "is".
الولد ← he، ولا ننسى is.
3
They is my parents.
They are my parents.
They goes with are.
مع they نستخدم are.
