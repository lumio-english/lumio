Did you know?
The long a in cake, rain and May sounds the same, but it is spelled a-e, ai and ay. And the chant does not work for every word: said has ai but sounds like e! That is why English spelling needs detectives.
الصوت الطويل a في cake و rain و May واحد، لكنه يُكتب a-e و ai و ay. لهذا تحتاج التهجئة إلى محققين!
