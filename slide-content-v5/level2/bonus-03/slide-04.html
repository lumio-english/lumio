Arabic and English vowels: cousins, not twins

كسرة: بِshort i: close to kasrakit, pin, sit
ضمة: بُshort u is NOT damma: a quick 'uh'cub, sun, cup

Arabic madd = the same vowel, held longer. English long vowels say the letter name.
