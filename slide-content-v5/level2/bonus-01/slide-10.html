A Greek letter in English words
φ → ph
phone, photo and elephant come from Greek. Greek had one letter for this sound: φ (phi).
  • alphabet = alpha + beta, the first two Greek letters
  • Arabic abjad works the same way: alif, ba, jim, dal!
