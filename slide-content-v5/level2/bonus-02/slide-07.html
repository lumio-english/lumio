Why does the Sneaky Vowel come?
ka · ta · ba
Every Standard Arabic syllable starts with ONE consonant and a vowel. Two consonants together at the start? Not allowed!
  • Arabic adds a helper vowel: اِسْم (hamzat al-wasl)
  • Your brain does the same in English: i-sport!
لا تبدأ الكلمة العربية الفصيحة بساكن، لذلك نضيف همزة الوصل: اِسْم.
