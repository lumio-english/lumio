Verb + -ing = a noun (an activity) · Find the mistake, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I like draw.
I like drawing.
After like: verb + -ing.
بعد like نضيف -ing للفعل.
2
She like singing.
She likes singing.
She -> likes.
مع she نقول likes.
3
Do you like cooking? Yes, I like.
Do you like cooking? Yes, I do.
Short answer: Yes, I do.
الجواب القصير: Yes, I do.
