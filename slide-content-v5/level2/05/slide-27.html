Common Mistakes

Possessive Adjectives: my, your, his, her, our, their · Find the mistake, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
This is me bag.
This is my bag.
Before a noun: my (not me).
قبل الاسم نستخدم my وليس me.
2
Omar is a boy. Her shirt is blue.
Omar is a boy. His shirt is blue.
A boy -> his.
الولد ← his.
3
This is the house of they.
This is their house.
Use their + noun.
نستخدم their + الاسم.
