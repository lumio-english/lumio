SENTENCE PATTERNS

I like...
“I like apples.”
“I like bananas.”
“I like juice.”
“I like milk.”
“I like cake.”
“I like ice cream.”
“I like pizza.”
“I like bread.”
“I like apples. I like bananas.”
“I like juice, milk, and pizza.”
“I like cake. I like ice cream.”

DIALOGUE

“Do you like apples?”
“Yes, I like apples! I like juice too.”
“I like pizza and ice cream.”
“Me too! Yummy!”
