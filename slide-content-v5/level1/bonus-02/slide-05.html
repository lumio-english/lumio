Hand on your throat!
zzz · sss
Your voice box has two tiny curtains. For buzz sounds they shake very fast. For whisper sounds they stay open.
  • zzz like a bee: your throat shakes
  • sss like a snake: only air, no buzz
  • Thumbs-up for buzz, flat hand for whisper
