SENTENCE PATTERNS

I am wearing... / It is a...
“It is a shirt.”
“It is a dress.”
“These are pants.”
“These are my shoes.”
“It is a hat.”
“It is a jacket.”
“These are socks.”
“It is a T-shirt.”
“Ziad wears a T-shirt and pants.”
“Sara wears a dress and shoes.”
“Omar wears a hat and a jacket.”

DIALOGUE

“What are you wearing?”
“I am wearing a shirt and pants.”
“I am wearing a T-shirt and shoes.”
“You look great!”
