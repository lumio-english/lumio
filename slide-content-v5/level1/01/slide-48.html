SENTENCE PATTERNS

This is + noun
“This is a boy.”
“This is a girl.”
“This is a teacher.”
“This is my friend.”
“This is my mom.”
“This is my dad.”
“The boy waves hello. The girl waves hello too.”
“My teacher teaches me. My friend plays with me.”
“My mom hugs me. My dad reads to me.”

DIALOGUE

“This is my friend.”
“Nice to meet you! Who is this?”
“This is my mom, and this is my dad.”
“Nice to meet your family!”
