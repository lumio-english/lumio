SENTENCE PATTERNS
Do / Does questions, daily routines, and how often: always, usually, rarely, never
“Does Noor even sleep? She wakes up at five to study.”
“Does she practice every day?”
“Do you wake up early?”
“He never skips practice. He loves it!”
“I usually wake up at seven.”
“We rarely watch TV on school days.”
“Does he wake up early?”
“Do you study every night?”
DIALOGUE
“Does Noor even sleep? She wakes up at five to study.”
“Then she practices piano, then she hangs out with us.”
“When does she relax?”
“She takes a nap after school. Ten minutes exactly.”
