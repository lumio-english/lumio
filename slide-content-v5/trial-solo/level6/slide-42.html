SENTENCE PATTERNS
Going to / will for plans and predictions, have to / must, and comparatives
“I'm going to plan a trip.”
“We're going to visit my cousins.”
“She's going to join the team.”
“He's going to apply for a job.”
“She wants to join the team.”
“I'm going to prepare for the exam.”
“We're going to practice every day.”
“She's going to organize her notes.”
“I need to organize my notes.”
“This book is more interesting than that one.”
“She's the most popular student.”
“I have to do my chores.”
“I have to be home by curfew.”
“My parents trust me.”
“I'm becoming more independent.”
DIALOGUE
“So what are you going to do after this year? Have you decided anything?”
“Honestly, I'm going to apply to a program abroad, if I'm brave enough.”
“That's huge. Are you going to move, or just visit for a while first?”
“Move eventually, I think, but I'm going to join a summer program first.”
