SENTENCE PATTERNS
Past Simple - I played / I went / Did you ...? / I didn't ...
“What did you do this weekend?”
“I played football yesterday.”
“We went to the mall last Friday.”
“Did you finish your homework?”
“I watched a movie last night.”
“We went to the mall on Friday.”
“I played football with my friends.”
“We watched a movie last night.”
DIALOGUE
“What did you do this weekend?”
“I played football, then I helped my dad and cleaned the car.”
“That's a chore, not a weekend.”
“After that I cooked dinner and watched two movies.”
