SENTENCE PATTERNS
Past Simple - I went / We saw / Did you...? - and telling a story in order
“I played football with my friends.”
“We watched a movie last night.”
“She walked to the park.”
“He helped his mom in the kitchen.”
“They cleaned their room.”
“My dad cooked dinner.”
“I packed my suitcase.”
“We flew to a new city.”
“We arrived at the airport.”
“There were a lot of guests.”
“There was a huge cake.”
“There were so many gifts.”
“I was proud of my grade.”
“She was nervous before the test.”
“I was surprised by the news.”
DIALOGUE
“So what did you actually do this weekend? Anything interesting happen?”
“Not really exciting, honestly. I played video games and watched a movie.”
“That sounds relaxing at least. I cleaned my whole room and cooked dinner.”
“Wow, productive weekend. Did anyone help you with all of that?”
