Common Mistakes
26 / 54
a / an · Find the mistake, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I like a apple.
I like an apple.
"apple" starts with a vowel sound -> an.
كلمة apple تبدأ بصوت علة ← نستخدم an.
2
An banana is yellow.
A banana is yellow.
"banana" starts with b -> a.
كلمة banana تبدأ بحرف b ← نستخدم a.
3
I eat egg for breakfast.
I eat an egg for breakfast.
One thing needs a / an.
الشيء الواحد يحتاج a أو an قبله.
