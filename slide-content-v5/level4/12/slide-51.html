Should / Shouldn't (advice) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
You should to sleep early.
You should sleep early.
No "to" after should.
لا نضع "to" بعد should.
2
He shoulds study more.
He should study more.
Should never changes.
should لا تتغير أبداً.
3
You don't should eat that.
You shouldn't eat that.
The negative is shouldn't.
النفي هو shouldn't.
