Countable & Uncountable Nouns · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
Can I have two waters?
Can I have two bottles of water?
Water is uncountable -- count the bottles or glasses.
الماء لا يُعد — نعدّ الزجاجات أو الأكواب.
2
She gave me many advices.
She gave me a lot of advice.
Advice has no plural.
advice ليس لها جمع.
3
I need some informations.
I need some information.
Information is uncountable.
information لا تُعد.
