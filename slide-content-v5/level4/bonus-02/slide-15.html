How English lost its plural 'you'
Long ago · thou
for one person: thou art
thou لشخص واحد
Long ago · ye / you
for more people, then the polite word for one
ye/you للجمع ثم للاحترام
Later · you
you replaced both ye and thou
حلّت you محل ye و thou
Today · you guys, y'all
people invent new plural forms

Language fixes its holes
Thou lives on only in old prayers, poems and a few northern English dialects.
