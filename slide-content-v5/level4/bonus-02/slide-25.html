Your bonus mission
Kitchen and Wallet Detective: list 10 things in your kitchen or your bag, write C or U next to each, and write 6 sentences using some, any, how much, how many and a piece of / a bottle of. Then write 3 sentences that use the trap words correctly (homework, advice, information).
محقق المطبخ والمحفظة: اكتب 10 أشياء في مطبخك أو حقيبتك، وضع C أو U بجانب كل منها، واكتب 6 جمل باستخدام some و any و how much و how many و a piece of / a bottle of. ثم اكتب 3 جمل تستخدم فيها كلمات الفخ بشكل صحيح (homework و advice و information).
  • List and label 10 nouns (C or U).
  • Write 6 sentences with some / any / how much / how many / units.
  • Write 3 correct sentences with homework, advice and information.
  • Bonus: ask a family member how many words for 'you' they know in other languages.
