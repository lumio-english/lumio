Present Simple Questions (Do / Does) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
Does she likes coffee?
Does she like coffee?
After "does" the verb is the base form.
بعد "does" يأتي الفعل الأساسي.
2
Do he play tennis?
Does he play tennis?
He, she, it → does.
مع he و she و it ← does.
3
You like pizza?
Do you like pizza?
Yes/no questions start with do/does.
أسئلة نعم/لا تبدأ بـ do/does.
