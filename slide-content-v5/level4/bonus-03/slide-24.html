Did you know?
Good, better, best come from completely different old words. Linguists call this suppletion. Arabic has something similar: for جيّد we often say أفضل, from a different root.
good و better و best جاءت من كلمات قديمة مختلفة، وفي العربية نقول «جيّد» ثم «أفضل» من جذر آخر (ف ض ل).
