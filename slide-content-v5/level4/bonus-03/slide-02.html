Big idea
English builds many new words like Lego, by adding pieces to the front and back of a word (happy → unhappy → unhappiness); Arabic weaves vowels and extra letters around a root (usually three letters) (ك ت ب → كاتب، مكتب، مكتبة). Know the pieces and you can understand words you have never seen.
