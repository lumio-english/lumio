Meaning and word class
Ask: what job does the sentence need here? A noun, an adjective or a verb?
  • Prefixes change the meaning: happy → unhappy, write → rewrite
  • -ful and -less make adjectives: helpful, careless
  • -ness, -ion and -ment make nouns: kindness, improvement
  • -er makes a person: teacher, player
