Morpheme: the smallest piece with meaning
un + help + ful
  • un- = not · help = the base · -ful = full of
  • cat = 1 morpheme · cats = 2 (cat + plural -s)
  • Careful: under is NOT un + der. 'der' means nothing!
المورفيم أصغر قطعة في الكلمة لها معنى: unhelpful = un + help + ful.
