Adding -er and -est
Rule 1 · double
one short vowel + one consonant: big → biggest, hot → hottest
نضاعف الحرف الساكن
Rule 2 · y → i
consonant + y: happy → happiest, funny → funniest
نحوّل y إلى i
Rule 3 · drop the e
silent e: nice → nicest, large → largest
نضيف -r أو -st فقط
