Reading Lab · Choosing a Club

This term, every student at Noor's school chooses a club. There are three options: robotics, football and Arabic calligraphy. Noor would like to try all of them, but she can only choose one.

She makes a list of pros and cons. Robotics is exciting, and her cousin says the teacher is great. But the club meets on Thursdays, and Noor has Qur'an class on Thursdays. Football is fun, but the club meets every day, and she doesn't want to play every day. Calligraphy is calm. Her grandmother writes beautiful letters, and Noor would like to learn.

Her friends have opinions. "Robotics!" says Sara. "We can build a robot together." Noor considers it. But there is a risk. Robotics means no Qur'an class on Thursdays, and Noor doesn't want any regrets.

In the end, Noor chooses calligraphy, and she asks the robotics teacher one question: "Can I visit the robotics room at break time?" She says yes. That is her compromise. Two months later, her first word in beautiful letters hangs on the kitchen wall. It says 'patience'.
