Common Mistakes

Object Pronouns · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
Please help I with this.
Please help me with this.
After a verb use me, not I.
بعد الفعل نستخدم me وليس I.
2
I saw she at the mall.
I saw her at the mall.
She is the subject; her is the object.
she فاعل، أما her فمفعول به.
3
Give the book to they.
Give the book to them.
After "to" use them.
بعد "to" نستخدم them.
