Adverbs of Frequency · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I go always to school by bus.
I always go to school by bus.
The adverb goes BEFORE the main verb.
الظرف يأتي قبل الفعل الرئيسي.
2
She always is late.
She is always late.
With "be", the adverb goes AFTER am/is/are.
مع فعل "be" يأتي الظرف بعد am/is/are.
3
He doesn't never eat fish.
He never eats fish.
Never is already negative -- no "doesn't".
never نفي بالفعل — لا نضيف "doesn't".
