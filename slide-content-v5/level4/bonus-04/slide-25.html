Why can 'Give me water, please' sound rude in English but عطني ماي لو سمحت is polite in Arabic?
Arabic makes a direct imperative polite with formulas like لو سمحت; English needs an indirect question or modal (Could I have...?), so an imperative + please still sounds like an order.
