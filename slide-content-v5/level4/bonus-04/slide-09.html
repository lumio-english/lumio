Did you know?
Could and would began as the past forms of can and will. English uses them to sound softer: Could you... is softer than Can you.... Many linguists think distance in time feels like polite distance.
could و would كانتا صيغتي الماضي من can و will، ويرى كثير من اللغويين أن البعد في الزمن يوحي بمسافة مهذّبة.
