Big idea
Politeness is built differently in each language: Arabic often makes a direct request polite with formulas (لو سمحت، من فضلك, and in spoken Arabic الله يخليك), while English makes it polite with indirect questions and modals (Could you..., Would you mind..., I'd like...). So a word-for-word translation like 'Give me water' can sound rude in English, even with please.
