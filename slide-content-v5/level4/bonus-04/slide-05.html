The tools of English politeness
  • An order becomes a question: Can you...?
  • can → could · will → would (softer)
  • Add please and softeners: just, a bit, maybe
  • I want → I'd like
  • Would you mind + -ing (not: to pass)
الأمر يصبح سؤالاً، و can تصبح could، ونضيف please و just و a bit.
