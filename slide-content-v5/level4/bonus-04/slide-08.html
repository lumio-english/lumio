Pragmatics: what people really mean
  • Words ask, promise, warn and thank: speech acts
  • 'Can you pass the salt?' = a request, not a question
  • Nobody answers 'Yes, I can' and does nothing!
  • 'It's a bit cold in here.' = Please close the window.
