Superlatives (the biggest, the best) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
He is the most tall boy in class.
He is the tallest boy in class.
Short adjectives → -est.
الصفات القصيرة ← -est.
2
This is the goodest movie.
This is the best movie.
Good → best (irregular).
good ← best (شاذة).
3
She is biggest fan of the team.
She is the biggest fan of the team.
Superlatives need "the".
صيغة التفضيل تحتاج "the".
