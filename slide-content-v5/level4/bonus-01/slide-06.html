The -s lives in only one place
Does he play?
  • He plays. → Does he play? (not: Does he plays?)
  • He plays. → He doesn't play. (not: doesn't plays)
  • Spelling: study → studies, watch → watches, go → goes
في السؤال والنفي تقفز -s إلى does، ويعود الفعل إلى أصله.
