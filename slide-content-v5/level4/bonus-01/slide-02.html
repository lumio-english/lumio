Today I can...
  1. 1
    I can explain why only he/she/it gets -s in the present simple.
    أستطيع أن أشرح لماذا يأخذ he/she/it وحدها حرف -s في المضارع البسيط.
  2. 2
    I can say the three sounds of -s: /s/ (works), /z/ (plays), /ɪz/ (watches).
    أستطيع أن أنطق أصوات -s الثلاثة: /s/ و /z/ و /ɪz/.
  3. 3
    I can choose at, on or in for any time expression using the Time Triangle.
    أستطيع أن أختار at أو on أو in لأي تعبير زمني باستخدام مثلث الوقت.
  4. 4
    I can find and fix the typical mistakes: 'He go', 'Does he plays?', 'in Monday'.
    أستطيع أن أكتشف وأصحّح الأخطاء الشائعة مثل He go و Does he plays? و in Monday.
Big idea
English verbs once had an ending for almost every person, like Arabic verbs do; today only he/she/it keeps one (-s), and it has three sounds. For time, think of a triangle: at a point, on a day, in a longer period.
