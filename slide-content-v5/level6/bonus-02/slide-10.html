Sara invites Noor to a brunch. Noor is busy.
One question: is it forbidden, or just not necessary?
  • Sara thinks in Arabic: مش لازم تيجي، عادي (not necessary).
  • Word by word: مش = not, لازم = must... 'You mustn't come!'
  • Noor thinks Sara doesn't want her there!
  • Sara meant: You don't have to come. It's OK.
السؤال الوحيد: هل الأمر ممنوع أم غير ضروري فقط؟
