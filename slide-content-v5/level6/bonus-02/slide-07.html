Grammar lab · must or have to?
7 / 27
Both mean 'necessary'. The difference is small.
FormOften used forExample
mustmy own rule or a written ruleI must call my grandma. I miss her.
mustnotices and signsVisitors must sign in.
have to / has toa rule from outsideI have to wear a uniform. It's the rule.
had tothe past of bothYesterday I had to study.
A tendency, not a strict rule. In American speech, have to is much more common.
هذا ميل عام لا قاعدة صارمة، و have to أكثر شيوعاً في الكلام الأمريكي.
