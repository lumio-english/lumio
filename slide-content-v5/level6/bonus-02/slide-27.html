Your bonus mission
My Ideal School Rulebook: write 10 rules for your dream school: 4 have to/must, 3 mustn't, 3 don't have to. Use a different adverb in at least 5 rules and check the spelling family of each adverb.
كتاب قواعد مدرستي المثالية: اكتب 10 قواعد لمدرسة أحلامك: 4 بـ have to/must، و3 بـ mustn't، و3 بـ don't have to. استخدم ظرفاً مختلفاً في 5 قواعد على الأقل وتحقق من قاعدة إملائه.
  • Write the 10-rule rulebook.
  • Photograph or copy two real signs and rewrite them as full English rules.
  • Write 3 sentences with 'don't have to' about your weekend (I don't have to wake up early on Friday).
