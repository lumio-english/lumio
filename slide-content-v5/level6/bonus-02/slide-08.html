must is a modal, like will. have to is a normal verb.
-s
No -s
She must go. NOT she musts. (But: she has to.)
لا تأخذ -s
to
No 'to' after it
I must finish. NOT I must to finish.
لا to بعدها
-ed
No past form
Yesterday I had to study. NOT I must study.
لا ماضي لها: نستخدم had to
?
No do in questions
Must I go? (But: Do I have to go?)
