Did you know?
Must was once a past tense! In Old English, motan meant 'may, be allowed to', and its past form moste became today's must. Could, would, should and might were past forms too.
كانت must فعلاً ماضياً: motan يعني 'يُسمح له'، وماضيه moste صار must. وكذلك could و would و should و might.
