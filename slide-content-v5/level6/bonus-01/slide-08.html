Did you know?
In Old English, willan meant 'to want' (we still say free will). 'Going to' first meant real movement: I am going to London. Slowly they became grammar words. Linguists call this grammaticalization.
كان willan يعني 'يريد'، و going to تعني الذهاب الحقيقي، ثم صارتا كلمتين نحويتين: 'التحوّل النحوي'.
