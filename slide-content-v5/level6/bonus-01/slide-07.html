Does English have a future tense?
Say: I think English has / doesn't have a future tense because...
  • NO: a tense is a verb ending. English has two: present and past.
  • YES: many school grammars call will + verb 'the future tense'.
  • Both sides have a point: it depends on what 'tense' means.
  • Vote: thumbs up = no future tense, thumbs down = yes!
