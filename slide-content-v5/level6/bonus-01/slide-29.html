Did you know?
Some languages, such as Mandarin Chinese, have no tense endings at all, not even for the past. They use time words like 'yesterday' and 'tomorrow' and small helper words, and people talk about the future perfectly well!
في الصينية لا توجد نهايات للأزمنة أصلاً، حتى للماضي، بل كلمات مثل 'أمس' و'غداً' وكلمات مساعدة.
