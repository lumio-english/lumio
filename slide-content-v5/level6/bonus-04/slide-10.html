Arabic thinking, English mistakes
1
I agree.
NOT I am agree. موافق works like an adjective; agree is a verb.
agree فعل وليست صفة
2
Although it's expensive, it's useful.
One linking word only: although OR but, never both.
بالرغم من... إلا أن: أداة واحدة في الإنجليزية
3
We discussed the problem.
discuss + object, no 'about'. (But: talk about.)
ناقش حول ← discuss بلا about
