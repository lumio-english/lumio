Have to / Don't have to · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
She have to wake up early.
She has to wake up early.
She → has to.
مع she ← has to.
2
You don't have to not come.
You don't have to come.
One negative only: don't have to.
نفي واحد فقط: don't have to.
3
I must to finish my homework.
I have to finish my homework.
Must has no "to"; have to does.
must لا تأتي مع "to"، أما have to فتأتي معها.
