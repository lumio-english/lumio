Did you know?
'Check' in chess comes from Persian shāh, 'king'. 'Checkmate' comes from shāh māt, which reached Europe through Arabic and French. Experts disagree whether māt is Persian ('stuck') or Arabic ('dead').
'كش مات' أصلها الفارسية 'شاه مات'، ووصلت إلى أوروبا عبر العربية والفرنسية.
