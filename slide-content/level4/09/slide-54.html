Reading Lab · Saving for Riyadh Season

Every year, Riyadh Season opens in October, and every year Ziad wants to go. There are games, concerts and a Ferris wheel taller than his school. But this year, he wants to pay for the ticket himself.

So Ziad has a plan. He has a goal jar on his shelf. Every week, he puts some money inside: coins from his pocket, notes from Eid, and a little money he earns from washing his father's car. He rarely spends anything now.
