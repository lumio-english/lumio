The class trip to the old souq is over, and the bus home is a mess. Things are everywhere. A jacket is on the floor, a lunchbox is under a seat, and someone's earbuds are hanging from the window.

"Whose jacket is this?" the teacher asks. "It's mine," says Omar. "No, it isn't," says Hamad. "Yours is black. This one is blue, so it's his." He points at Ziad. Ziad looks at the jacket and shakes his head. "Mine has a hood."

Then Sara finds a notebook with a falcon sticker. "This is Noor's," she says. "It's hers; I know her stickers." Noor is happy. Her notes about the souq are inside: the spices, the carpets, the man who sells dates from a wooden cart.

In the end, the blue jacket belongs to the bus driver. Everyone laughs. The earbuds are Ziad's, the lunchbox is Hamad's, and the umbrella is nobody's. "Then it's ours now," the teacher says. "It rains in winter!"
