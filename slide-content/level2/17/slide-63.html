SENTENCE PATTERNS
Do you like...? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
“I like drawing but my sister likes writing stories.”
“She likes singing and she sings every morning.”
“He likes reading books about animals and space.”
“We like dancing when our favorite song plays.”
“My mom likes cooking and I like eating what she makes!”
“I like painting pictures of my family and my pets.”
“I like drawing. I like painting.”
“I like singing. I like dancing.”
“I like reading. I like cooking.”
GRAMMAR
Verb + -ing = a noun (an activity)
“I like drawing.”
“Do you like singing?”
“Reading is fun.”
“Cooking is my favorite.”
DIALOGUE
“Do you like drawing?”
“Yes, I do! I like drawing and painting.”
“Do you like singing?”
“No, I don't. But I like dancing!”
