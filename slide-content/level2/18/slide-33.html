Reading Lab · At the Dates Market

Noor and her dad are at the dates market in Buraidah. It is the biggest dates market in the world! There are boxes everywhere.

"Look at these dates," says Noor. "They are golden!" "Those dates are sukkari," says the seller. "They are soft and very sweet. Try one!"

Noor tries one. Mmm! Then she points at a box far away. "What are those?" "Those are ajwa dates from Madinah," says her dad. "They are dark and small."

"This box, please," says Noor's dad. The seller puts the dates in a bag. "And this is for you," the seller says to Noor. It is one more date. Noor smiles. "Thank you!"
