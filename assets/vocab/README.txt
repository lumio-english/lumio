TRANSPARENT-BACKGROUND IMAGES FOR VOCABULARY
=============================================
Drop an image here named after the English word (lowercase, spaces -> dashes):
  apple.png      good-morning.png      ice-cream.png

Format: PNG with a transparent background (no background at all, not even
white). Any image you add -- AI-generated or a real photo -- gets its
background automatically removed and gets cropped to content before being
saved here, so it composites cleanly onto the site's cream/orange cards,
flashcards, and story scenes without a visible white box around it.

- The WEBSITE and the in-dashboard PRESENTATION use them instantly (no rebuild).
- Flashcard PDFs need a rebuild after adding/changing images -- ask Claude to
  rerun the flashcards generator for the affected level.
- For the PPTX files, ask Claude to rebuild the decks after adding photos.
