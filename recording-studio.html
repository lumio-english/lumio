For a good recording

  1. A quiet room with soft things around (curtains, a sofa). No fan or AC noise.
  2. Phone or headset mic about a hand's width from your mouth.
  3. Same voice, same distance, same energy for every line.
  4. Leave a short pause before and after. The studio trims silence and evens out the volume for you.
  5. Do one level at a time; Pre-A is first because the youngest children hear it most.
