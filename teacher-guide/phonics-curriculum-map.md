# Phonics Curriculum Map — Level 1 & Level 2

This is the full phonics scope and sequence, and exactly which lesson
each phonics unit is now taught in inside the Present decks. Every
unit below gets two slides inside its lesson: a **"Phonics Time!"**
slide (teacher explains the rule, sounds, and the Arabic tip) and a
**"Listen & Spot"** slide (kids tap letter-tile words to practice
reading/blending them). The full reference — same content, browsable
any time — also lives in the Phonics tab of the English Hub.

Placement rule: unit *N* is taught in **Lesson (2N-1)** of its level,
so phonics shows up every other lesson, alternating with a normal
vocab-only lesson in between. This keeps every phonics lesson tied
to a specific point in the course instead of being a separate track
kids might skip.

## Why this order

It follows the same progression as Jolly Phonics / Letters and
Sounds (single letter-sounds → CVC blending → sight words → digraphs
→ blends → vowel teams → more sight words), just spread across the
existing 20-lesson course instead of being a separate 17-lesson
track. Level 1 covers single-letter sounds through first blending;
Level 2 covers everything a Level 1 grad needs to start reading real
sentences — digraphs, blends, and vowel teams.

## Not covered yet (flagged in the earlier curriculum-plan doc too)

Silent e (a_e, i_e, o_e), r-controlled vowels (ar, er, ir, or, ur),
remaining vowel teams (oo, ow, ou, oi, oy), and spelling suffixes
(-ing, -ed, -s/-es rules) are the natural Level 3+ continuation once
this rollout is confirmed working well in class.
