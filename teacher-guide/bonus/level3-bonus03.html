The Question Machine

آلة الأسئلة
Big idea: English builds a question by moving a helper verb (is, are, can) in front of the subject; if there is no helper, it calls do or does. Arabic just adds هل or changes the tone.
تصنع الإنجليزية السؤال بنقل فعل مساعد (is, are, can) إلى ما قبل الفاعل، وإن لم يوجد فعل مساعد تستدعي do أو does. أما العربية فتضيف هل أو تغيّر نغمة الصوت فقط.

Objectives

I can turn a statement into a yes/no question by moving the helper (Noor is shy -> Is Noor shy?).أستطيع أن أحوّل الجملة الخبرية إلى سؤال بنعم أو لا بنقل الفعل المساعد (Noor is shy ← Is Noor shy?).
I can use do / does when there is no helper, and remove the -s from the main verb (Does she play...?).أستطيع أن أستعمل do / does عندما لا يوجد فعل مساعد، وأحذف -s من الفعل الأساسي (Does she play...?).
I can build Wh- questions in the right order: Wh- + helper + subject + verb.أستطيع أن أبني أسئلة Wh- بالترتيب الصحيح: أداة الاستفهام + الفعل المساعد + الفاعل + الفعل.
I can use rising and falling intonation and compare English questions with Arabic هل questions.أستطيع أن أستعمل النغمة الصاعدة والهابطة، وأقارن الأسئلة الإنجليزية بأسئلة هل في العربية.

Linguistics Corner — How the world's languages ask questions · كيف تسأل لغات العالم

Languages use three main tools to ask a yes/no question: a question word or particle (Arabic هل, Chinese ma, Japanese ka, Turkish mi), intonation alone (the voice goes up), or changing the word order (English, German).تستعمل اللغات ثلاث أدوات رئيسية لطرح سؤال جوابه نعم أو لا: أداة استفهام (هل في العربية، و ma في الصينية، و ka في اليابانية، و mi في التركية)، أو النغمة وحدها (يرتفع الصوت)، أو تغيير ترتيب الكلمات (الإنجليزية والألمانية).
The particle method is the most common in the world. English inversion is less common, and English 'do-support' (a helper with no meaning of its own) is rare among the world's languages.طريقة أداة الاستفهام هي الأكثر انتشاراً في العالم. أما قلب الترتيب في الإنجليزية فأقل انتشاراً، واستدعاء do (فعل مساعد بلا معنى خاص به) نادر بين لغات العالم.
English did not always use do. About 400 years ago, Shakespeare could write 'Know you the way?' and 'What say you?'. Over the next century or so, do became the normal helper: Do you know the way? What do you say?لم تكن الإنجليزية تستعمل do دائماً. قبل نحو 400 سنة كان شكسبير يكتب 'Know you the way?' و'What say you?'، ثم صارت do خلال القرن التالي تقريباً هي الفعل المساعد المعتاد: Do you know the way?
In most languages, including English and Arabic, the voice often rises at the end of a yes/no question. In English, Wh- questions usually fall at the end. Intonation is grammar you hear, not grammar you see.في معظم اللغات، ومنها الإنجليزية والعربية، يرتفع الصوت غالباً في آخر السؤال الذي جوابه نعم أو لا. وفي الإنجليزية تنخفض النغمة عادة في آخر أسئلة Wh-. التنغيم قواعد نسمعها ولا نراها.

الإنجليزية تنقل can قبل you. في العامية قد تكفي النغمة وحدها لصنع السؤال، أما الإنجليزية فتحتاج إلى الفعل المساعد في البداية.

العربية: أداة الاستفهام + الفعل. الإنجليزية: أداة الاستفهام + do + الفاعل + الفعل. لا يوجد في العربية ما يقابل do هنا، لذلك ينساها الطلاب.

النمط نفسه! عندما تكون who هي الفاعل لا تستعمل الإنجليزية do، تماماً كالعربية.

Common errors of Arabic speakers

Student saysCorrectWhy
Where you go after school?Where do you go after school?Copy of أين تذهب؟ Arabic has no word for do in questions.
You can juggle?Can you juggle?Copy of the Arabic statement order with question tone. Native speakers say this only to show surprise or check; a normal question moves can to the front.
Why he is nervous?Why is he nervous?The helper stays in statement order. After the Wh- word, the helper must jump before the subject.
Does she plays tennis?Does she play tennis?The -s moves to does. One -s per sentence: does takes it, the main verb goes back to base form.
Do he like puzzles?Does he like puzzles?he / she / it need does, just like doesn't in negatives (Lesson 3).
What means riddle?What does riddle mean?Copy of ماذا تعني؟ The verb comes straight after the question word in Arabic; English needs does first.

1. Warm-up: 20 Questions with Lumi

0–5 min
إحماء: عشرون سؤالاً مع لومي
Method: Guessing game (authentic need to ask questions)
Purpose: Get students asking questions freely so the teacher can collect their real question forms, correct and incorrect.
Teacher says

“Lumi has a mystery object in this box. You can ask yes/no questions only. I answer yes or no.”

“Ready? Ask me anything! Is it big? Can you eat it? Go!”

“(Write every student question on the slide exactly as they say it, even the broken ones.)”

“You found it! Now look at all your questions. Some are perfect, some are... interesting. Today we build a Question Machine so every question comes out perfect.”

  • اسألوا أسئلة جوابها نعم أو لا فقط.
  • انظروا إلى أسئلتكم: بعضها ممتاز وبعضها يحتاج إلى إصلاح. اليوم نبني آلة أسئلة.
Students do
  • Ask yes/no questions to find the mystery object (a backpack, a medal or a controller).
  • Look at the list of their own questions on the slide.
Tips:
  • Do not correct during the game. Write the questions down; they become the material for the Discovery stage.
  • Typical forms you will collect: 'It is big?', 'You can eat it?', 'Is it blue?' - perfect contrast material.

2. Discovery: How the Question Machine Works

5–17 min
اكتشاف: كيف تعمل آلة الأسئلة
Method: Guided discovery (find-the-rule) with a slot diagram and moving helper cards
Purpose: Students discover inversion: the helper jumps over the subject in yes/no and Wh- questions.
Teacher says

“Look at the pairs. Statement: 'Noor is nervous.' Question: 'Is Noor nervous?' What moved? ... Is! Where did it go? ... In front of Noor!”

“Another: 'Ziad can code.' -> 'Can Ziad code?' Same thing! The yellow helper jumps over the subject.”

“'They are friendly.' -> ? ... 'Are they friendly?' Perfect.”

“Now Wh- questions. Put the Wh- word at the very front, then the machine works the same: 'Where is the gym?' 'What can Omar bake?'”

“The machine has five slots: Wh-word, helper, subject, verb, rest. The Wh- slot can be empty for yes/no questions.”

“Now go back to our 20 Questions list. 'It is big?' - fix it with the machine! ... 'Is it big?'”

  • ما الكلمة التي تحركت؟ الفعل المساعد قفز فوق الفاعل!
  • في أسئلة Wh- نضع أداة الاستفهام أولاً، ثم تعمل الآلة بالطريقة نفسها.
Students do
  • Compare statement/question pairs and say what moved.
  • Move the yellow helper card in front of the subject on paper (or tell the teacher where to drag it).
  • Fix their own warm-up questions using the machine.
  • Do the transformation exercise.
Exercise · transformation — Make a question. Move the helper in front of the subject. (Wh- word given in brackets.)
كوّن سؤالاً بنقل الفعل المساعد قبل الفاعل. (أداة الاستفهام بين القوسين.)
  1. The new kid is shy. → Is the new kid shy?
  2. Sara can draw anime characters. → Can Sara draw anime characters?
  3. They are in the science lab. → Are they in the science lab?
  4. Hamad is faster than Omar. → Is Hamad faster than Omar?
  5. You can solve the riddle. → Can you solve the riddle?
  6. (Where) The clue is ___. → Where is the clue?
  7. (What) Ziad can bake ___. → What can Ziad bake?
  8. (Why) The newcomer is nervous. → Why is the newcomer nervous?
  9. (Who) The suspect is ___. → Who is the suspect?
  10. (How) You are ___. → How are you?
Tips:
  • Physically moving the helper card is the key: it makes inversion visible. If students cannot control slides, they say 'move is to the front!'
  • Point out that the helper always comes right after the Wh- word: Wh- + helper + subject.

3. The Helper Who Appears: Do and Does

17–27 min
المساعد الذي يظهر فجأة: do و does
Method: Problem-solving discovery + controlled practice
Purpose: Discover that when there is no helper, English calls do / does, and the -s moves from the verb to does.
Teacher says

“Problem! 'You like riddles.' Where's the yellow helper? ... There isn't one! Like is the main verb. Can we move like? 'Like you riddles?' ... Sounds like Shakespeare!”

“So the machine calls a helper: DO. 'Do you like riddles?'”

“Now: 'Sara plays tennis.' The machine calls does for she. And look at plays... the -s jumps onto does! 'Does Sara play tennis?'”

“One -s per sentence: does takes it, play goes back to normal.”

“Same in Wh- questions: 'Where do you hang out?' 'What does Hamad collect?'”

“You already know this helper! Lesson 3: I don't, she doesn't. Do and does work in negatives and questions.”

  • لا يوجد فعل مساعد؟ الآلة تستدعي do. ومع he / she / it تستدعي does.
  • حرف -s ينتقل إلى does، ويعود الفعل الأساسي إلى أصله.
Students do
  • Try to move the main verb, notice it sounds wrong, then add do / does.
  • Show the 'do' or 'does' card for each subject the teacher says (I, she, they, my cousin, Omar and Ziad).
  • Complete the exercise and read each question aloud.
Exercise · choose-the-helper — Make a question. Choose is, are, can, do or does. Watch the -s!
كوّن سؤالاً. اختر is أو are أو can أو do أو does، وانتبه لحرف -s!
  1. You like puzzles. → Do you like puzzles?
  2. Noor plays basketball. → Does Noor play basketball?
  3. Your cousins live near school. → Do your cousins live near school?
  4. Omar can juggle. → Can Omar juggle?
  5. (Where) You hang out ___. → Where do you hang out?
  6. (What) Hamad collects ___. → What does Hamad collect?
  7. The champion is confident. → Is the champion confident?
  8. (When) The race starts ___. → When does the race start?
  9. (What) 'Belong' means ___. → What does 'belong' mean?
  10. (Why) They need a hint. → Why do they need a hint?
Tips:
  • If a student says 'Does she plays?', point at the -s and say 'Only one -s allowed!'
  • Challenge for fast finishers: 'Who won the race?' Why is there no do? (who is the subject; see Arabic Bridge).
  • Keep the 'Shakespeare' joke: 'Like you riddles?' sounds old because it IS old English.

4. Linguistics Corner: How the World Asks

27–34 min
ركن اللغويات: كيف يسأل العالم
Method: Mini-lecture + 'which tool?' quiz
Purpose: Broaden general knowledge: particles, intonation and inversion across languages, and the history of do.
Teacher says

“Languages have three tools to ask: a question word like هل, the voice going up, or moving words around.”

“Arabic uses هل. Chinese adds ma at the end. Japanese adds ka. English? Moves the helper!”

“The particle tool is the most common in the world. English's do-helper is quite rare - you are learning an unusual system.”

“And English didn't always use do! Shakespeare, 400 years ago: 'Know you the way?' Today: 'Do you know the way?'”

  • اللغات تسأل بثلاث أدوات: أداة استفهام مثل هل، أو رفع الصوت، أو تحريك الكلمات.
  • استعمال do في الأسئلة نادر بين لغات العالم، وكان شكسبير قبل 400 سنة يسأل بدونها.
Students do
  • Decide which tool each example uses.
  • Turn two Shakespeare-style questions into modern English.
Exercise · which-tool — Which tool makes the question? Write: particle, intonation or word order. For Shakespeare, write the modern question.
ما الأداة التي تصنع السؤال؟ اكتب: أداة استفهام أو نغمة أو ترتيب. ولأسئلة شكسبير اكتب السؤال الحديث.
  1. هل تحب الألغاز؟ → particle (هل)
  2. Chinese: Ni hao ma? (You good ma?) → particle (ma)
  3. Can you code? → word order (inversion)
  4. Spoken Arabic: بتعرف تعوم؟ → intonation
  5. Japanese: ...desu ka? → particle (ka)
  6. Shakespeare: Know you the answer? → Do you know the answer?
  7. Shakespeare: What say you? → What do you say?
Tips:
  • Keep it light; the history is a hook, not a test.
  • 'English uses inversion' is also true in German (Spielst du?) - mention if a student knows German or French.

5. Arabic Bridge and Voice Music: هل vs the Machine

34–43 min
جسر العربية وموسيقى الصوت: هل مقابل الآلة
Method: Contrastive analysis + intonation drill with arrow cards
Purpose: Translate Arabic questions using the machine, and practise rising (yes/no) and falling (Wh-) intonation.
Teacher says

“Arabic: هل + the normal sentence. Nothing moves. English: the helper moves or do appears.”

“Interesting: هل has no meaning of its own - it only says 'question!'. English do in a question is the same: an empty helper.”

“Careful with أين الصالة الرياضية؟ - no verb in Arabic. English needs is: Where IS the gym? Bonus Class 1 again!”

“Now the music. Yes/no questions usually go UP at the end: Can you SWIM? (arrow up). Wh- questions usually go DOWN: Where do you LIVE? (arrow down).”

“Show me your arrow when I say a question!”

  • العربية تضع هل قبل الجملة ولا تحرّك شيئاً، والإنجليزية تحرّك الفعل المساعد أو تستدعي do.
  • أسئلة نعم/لا ترتفع نغمتها في الآخر غالباً، وأسئلة Wh- تنخفض غالباً.
Students do
  • Translate Arabic questions into English with the machine.
  • Hold up the up or down arrow for each question the teacher reads.
  • Read their own translations with the right arrow.
Exercise · translate-and-intonate — Say it in English, then show the arrow: up or down.
قلها بالإنجليزية ثم ارفع السهم: صاعد أم هابط.
  1. هل تستطيع أن تتسلق؟ → Can you climb? (up)
  2. أين الصالة الرياضية؟ → Where is the gym? (down)
  3. هل يحب عمر الألغاز؟ → Does Omar like riddles? (up)
  4. ماذا تعني كلمة clue؟ → What does 'clue' mean? (down)
  5. هل الطالب الجديد ودود؟ → Is the new kid friendly? (up)
  6. متى يبدأ السباق؟ → When does the race start? (down)
  7. لماذا أنت متوتر؟ → Why are you nervous? (down)
  8. مَن فاز بالميدالية؟ → Who won the medal? (down; no do - who is the subject)
Tips:
  • Be honest about intonation: 'usually', not 'always'. Wh- questions can rise when you are surprised or being extra friendly.
  • Item 8 uses won (past) - students know 'won' from Lesson 11 vocabulary context; accept 'Who has the medal?' too.
  • Exaggerate the voice music at first, then make it natural.

6. Team Jeopardy: Here's the Answer, What's the Question?

43–54 min
تحدي الفرق: هذا هو الجواب، فما السؤال؟
Method: Jeopardy-style team game (reverse questions, gamification)
Purpose: Produce accurate questions under game pressure, choosing the right helper and order.
Teacher says

“Jeopardy time! Four categories: Can-Do, Crew Facts, Mystery and Race Day. 10 to 40 points.”

“I show you the ANSWER. You give me the QUESTION. Perfect question = full points. Right idea but a broken machine = half points, and the other team can steal by fixing it!”

“Team Lumi chooses first. Which category and how many points?”

  • أعرض عليكم الجواب، وأنتم تقولون السؤال.
  • السؤال الصحيح تماماً = كل النقاط. وإذا كان فيه خطأ يمكن للفريق الآخر أن يسرق النقاط بإصلاحه.
Students do
  • Choose a category and value.
  • Agree on the question with their teammate (15 seconds) and say it with the right intonation.
  • Steal points by fixing the other team's broken question.
Exercise · reverse-questions — Here is the answer. Say the question. (Model questions below; other correct questions are fine.)
هذا هو الجواب، فما السؤال؟ (أسئلة نموذجية أدناه، وتُقبل أسئلة صحيحة أخرى.)
  1. Can-Do 10: Yes, I can. I can swim fast. → Can you swim?
  2. Can-Do 20: No, she can't. Sara can't juggle. → Can Sara juggle?
  3. Crew Facts 10: Yes, he does. Omar likes memes. → Does Omar like memes?
  4. Crew Facts 20: We hang out at the mall. → Where do you hang out?
  5. Crew Facts 30: Hamad collects sneakers. → What does Hamad collect?
  6. Mystery 20: The suspect is the man in the black jacket. → Who is the suspect?
  7. Mystery 30: It means a small clue. → What does 'hint' mean?
  8. Mystery 40: Because she has a new secret. → Why is she happy? / Why does she smile? (any logical Why question)
  9. Race Day 20: Yes, he is. Ziad is faster than Omar. → Is Ziad faster than Omar?
  10. Race Day 40: The race starts at nine o'clock. → When does the race start? / What time does the race start?
Tips:
  • Short answers (Yes, he does / No, she can't) are a gift: the helper in the answer tells you the helper for the question. Teach this trick if students struggle.
  • With 2 students, play student vs student; with 1 student, student vs Lumi (the teacher plays Lumi and sometimes gives broken questions to steal).
  • 40-point items accept any logical question; reward creativity.

7. Exit Ticket, Did You Know? and Mission

54–60 min
بطاقة الخروج وهل تعلم؟ والمهمة
Method: Formative check + curiosity close
Purpose: Check each student's question building individually and send them home with a fact.
Teacher says

“Exit ticket: five questions to build, alone, in a private chat.”

“Did you know? Small children learning English as their first language also say 'Where you going?' and 'Why he is sad?' for a while. Your mistakes are the same ones English babies make - it's a natural step!”

“Your mission: interview someone at home with 5 English questions: one with is, one with can, one with do, one with does and one Wh- question.”

  • هل تعلم؟ الأطفال الصغار الذين يتعلمون الإنجليزية لغةً أولى يقولون أيضاً 'Where you going?' لفترة من الوقت. أخطاؤك مرحلة طبيعية!
  • مهمتك: اسأل أحداً في البيت خمسة أسئلة بالإنجليزية.
Students do
  • Build 5 questions in a private chat.
  • Read one question aloud with the right intonation.
Tips:
  • Note who still uses 'Where you...?' and add one question-building warm-up to Lessons 13-14.
  • The child-language fact is well documented (children go through stages of non-inverted questions); it lowers anxiety about errors.

Exit ticket

Exercise · exit ticket — Make the question.
كوّن السؤال.
  1. Noor is confident. (yes/no) → Is Noor confident?
  2. Ziad can code. (yes/no) → Can Ziad code?
  3. Sara studies after dinner. (yes/no) → Does Sara study after dinner?
  4. Fix it: Where you meet your friends? → Where do you meet your friends?
  5. Fix it: Why the new kid is nervous? → Why is the new kid nervous?

Homework

Interview Mission: ask someone at home 5 English questions (is, can, do, does, and a Wh- question). Write the questions and their answers. Then write a 5-question riddle about a mystery object for the next class.

مهمة المقابلة: اسأل أحداً في البيت خمسة أسئلة بالإنجليزية (is و can و do و does وسؤال Wh-) واكتب الأسئلة وإجاباتها. ثم اكتب لغزاً من 5 أسئلة عن شيء غامض للحصة القادمة.

  • Write 5 interview questions and the answers.
  • Colour the helper yellow and the subject blue in each question.
  • Prepare a mystery object for a 20 Questions game in Lesson 13.

Support

  • Work with is and can only first; add do / does after the student is confident.
  • Keep the five-slot Question Machine on screen for every task.
  • Give the helper card for each item in the transformation exercises.

Challenge

  • Subject questions: compare 'Who likes Omar?' and 'Who does Omar like?' What is the difference in meaning, and why does one have does?
  • Question tags: 'You can swim, can't you?' Find the pattern in 4 examples.
  • Write 3 Shakespeare-style questions and modernize them.

Success criteria

  • Makes yes/no questions with is / are / can by inversion in at least 8 of 10 items.
  • Chooses do or does correctly and drops the main-verb -s.
  • Uses the order Wh- + helper + subject + verb.
  • Uses rising intonation for yes/no questions and falling intonation for Wh- questions in a controlled drill.

Watch for

  • No inversion: Where you go? / Why he is...?
  • Statement order with rising tone as the only question signal: You can swim?
  • Double -s: Does she plays?
  • do with he / she / it
  • What means...? instead of What does ... mean?

Teacher notes

  • Questions are the one place where English breaks the strict S-V-O order taught in Bonus Class 2. Say this explicitly: 'English is strict, but questions have their own special order.'
  • Declarative questions ('You can swim?') do exist in real English for surprise or checking. Do not call them 'wrong'; call them 'not the normal question'.
  • Subject questions (Who won?) are a challenge point only; most students are not ready to contrast subject and object questions at this level.
  • Bonus Class 4 uses the same helpers (am / is / are) in the present continuous: Are you studying? What is she doing?
