Background and Action: Was Doing vs Did

الخلفية والحدث: was doing مقابل did
Big idea: A story has two layers: the background, filmed like a video (was/were + -ing: It was raining, people were waiting), and the events, taken like snapshots (past simple: the bus arrived). English chooses the form by how we look at the action, not only by when it happened.
للقصة طبقتان: الخلفية، وتُصوَّر كفيديو (was/were + -ing: كانت تمطر، كان الناس ينتظرون)، والأحداث، وتُلتقط كصور سريعة (الماضي البسيط: وصل الباص). الإنجليزية تختار الصيغة حسب طريقة نظرنا إلى الفعل، لا حسب وقت حدوثه فقط.

Linguistics Corner — Two cameras: tense and aspect · كاميرتان: الزمن والجهة

Linguists separate two ideas in a verb. Tense says WHEN (past or present). Aspect says HOW we look at the action: from outside as one complete dot, or from inside while it is still going on.يفرّق اللغويون بين فكرتين في الفعل: الزمن يقول «متى» (ماضٍ أو حاضر)، والجهة تقول «كيف ننظر إلى الفعل»: من الخارج كنقطة كاملة، أو من الداخل وهو ما زال مستمراً.
Past simple = a snapshot: the action is complete (The bus arrived). Past continuous = a video: we are inside the action, it has no start or end on screen (It was raining).الماضي البسيط = صورة فوتوغرافية: الفعل كامل (وصل الباص). الماضي المستمر = فيديو: نحن داخل الفعل، ولا نرى بدايته ولا نهايته (كانت تمطر).
Linguists who study stories in many languages (for example Paul Hopper, in 1979) noticed the same thing in language after language: storytellers separate the foreground (the main events, in order) from the background (the scene, the reasons, what was going on). Languages just use different tools to do it.لاحظ لغويون درسوا القصص في لغات كثيرة (مثل بول هوبر) الأمر نفسه في لغة بعد أخرى: الراوي يفصل بين المقدّمة (الأحداث الرئيسية بالترتيب) والخلفية (المشهد والأسباب وما كان يجري). اللغات تستخدم أدوات مختلفة فقط.
The same two actions can tell two different stories: 'When Sara arrived, we ate' (she came, THEN we ate). 'When Sara arrived, we were eating' (we started BEFORE she came). One small -ing changes the timeline.الفعلان نفساهما قد يحكيان قصتين مختلفتين: When Sara arrived, we ate (جاءت ثم أكلنا). When Sara arrived, we were eating (بدأنا قبل وصولها). حرف -ing صغير يغيّر الخط الزمني.
Some verbs do not like the video camera. State verbs (know, like, want, need, believe) describe a situation, not an activity, so we usually say 'I knew', not 'I was knowing'.بعض الأفعال لا تحب كاميرا الفيديو. أفعال الحالة (know, like, want, need, believe) تصف وضعاً لا نشاطاً، لذلك نقول عادة I knew وليس I was knowing.
Many linguists say Arabic ماضٍ and مضارع are also partly about aspect: ماضٍ often means a complete action, and مضارع an incomplete or ongoing one. That is why كان + مضارع (كان يلعب) can paint an action in progress in the past.يرى كثير من اللغويين أن الماضي والمضارع في العربية يتعلقان جزئياً بالجهة أيضاً: الماضي غالباً حدث مكتمل، والمضارع حدث غير مكتمل أو مستمر. ولهذا يرسم «كان + المضارع» (كان يلعب) فعلاً مستمراً في الماضي.

Arabic Bridge

EnglishالعربيةWhat to notice
He was playing football when it started to rain.كان يلعب كرة القدم عندما بدأ المطر.A great match: كان + المضارع ≈ was/were + -ing for an action in progress. Both need TWO pieces: كان + يلعب, was + playing.
تطابق رائع: كان + المضارع ≈ was/were + -ing لفعل مستمر في الماضي. وكلاهما يحتاج قطعتين: كان + يلعب، was + playing.
He played football every Friday. / He used to play football every Friday.كان يلعب كرة القدم كل يوم جمعة.Careful! The same Arabic form can also mean a past HABIT (something that happened again and again). For a past habit English usually does NOT use was playing; it uses the past simple or used to.
انتبه! الصيغة العربية نفسها قد تعني أيضاً عادة متكررة في الماضي. وللعادة في الماضي لا تستخدم الإنجليزية عادةً was playing، بل الماضي البسيط أو used to.
while / whenبينما / عندما، لمّاwhile usually goes with the long background action (while I was studying); when usually goes with the short event (when the phone rang). Arabic بينما and عندما work in a similar way.
while تأتي عادة مع الفعل الطويل في الخلفية (while I was studying)، و when مع الحدث القصير (when the phone rang). وتعمل «بينما» و«عندما» بطريقة مشابهة.
I was studying. (was + -ing)كنتُ أدرس.English has no single ending for 'in progress'; it builds it from be + -ing. Arabic speakers sometimes keep only one piece: 'I was study' or 'I studying'. Both pieces are needed.
لا توجد في الإنجليزية نهاية واحدة للاستمرار، بل تُبنى من be + -ing. وأحياناً يكتفي متحدثو العربية بقطعة واحدة: I was study أو I studying. القطعتان ضروريتان.

1. Warm-up: Freeze Frame

0–5 min
إحماء: تجميد الصورة
Method: Picture description + personal questions (activation)
Purpose: Revive was/were + -ing from Lesson 10 and feel the 'video' meaning before naming it.
Teacher says

“Look at the picture: a storm, a bus stop. I press PAUSE. What was happening at that moment? It was raining. People were waiting...”

“Now your life: what were you doing yesterday at 8 o'clock in the evening? And at 7 this morning?”

“Ziad says: 'At 8 pm I was studying for my exam. Suddenly, the lights went out!' Two kinds of verbs. Can you hear them?”

  • أوقفت الصورة. ماذا كان يحدث في تلك اللحظة؟
  • ماذا كنت تفعل أمس الساعة الثامنة مساءً؟
Students do
  • Describe the frozen picture with was/were + -ing (3-4 sentences together).
  • Answer 'What were you doing at...?' about their own day.
Tips:
  • Accept short answers; the goal is to warm up the form.
  • Write Ziad's two sentences on the slide; they are the anchor for Stage 2.

2. Linguistics Corner: The Video Camera and the Photo Camera

5–15 min
ركن اللغويات: كاميرا الفيديو وكاميرا الصور
Method: Guided discovery with a visual metaphor (concept checking questions)
Purpose: Discover tense vs aspect and the background/foreground roles in a story.
Teacher says

“Every storyteller has two cameras. The video camera films the background: it was raining, people were waiting. We are INSIDE the action. We don't see the start or the end.”

“The photo camera takes snapshots: the bus arrived, the lights went out. Click! Complete actions. They move the story forward.”

“Linguists have names for this. Tense = when: past. Aspect = how we look at it: from inside (was doing) or as a complete dot (did).”

“Concept check: 'It was raining.' Did the rain stop in this sentence? We don't know! 'It rained.' Is it complete? Yes.”

“Now read 'The Airport Day'. For each sentence, hold up your video camera or your photo camera.”

  • للراوي كاميرتان: كاميرا الفيديو تصوّر الخلفية ونحن داخل الحدث، وكاميرا الصور تلتقط أحداثاً كاملة تحرّك القصة.
  • الزمن = متى. الجهة = كيف ننظر إلى الحدث.
Students do
  • Listen to the two-camera explanation and answer the concept questions.
  • Read 'The Airport Day' and show the video (B) or photo (E) card for each sentence.
  • Find the pattern: which verb form is in every B sentence?
Exercise · classify — Is it background (B, video camera) or event (E, photo camera)?
هل هي خلفية (B، كاميرا الفيديو) أم حدث (E، كاميرا الصور)؟
  1. It was a hot morning, and hundreds of people were waiting at the airport. → B
  2. Sara and her family were standing in a long queue. → B
  3. Her little brother was playing a game on his phone. → B
  4. Suddenly, Sara checked her bag. → E
  5. She shouted, 'Where is my boarding pass?' → E
  6. She ran back to the café. → E
  7. A waiter was cleaning the tables and holding a small piece of paper. → B
  8. He smiled and gave her the boarding pass. Sara felt so relieved! → E
Tips:
  • Sentence 1 has 'It was a hot morning' (a state with was) and 'were waiting': both set the scene. The test is the sentence's job in the story: does it set the scene (B) or move the story forward (E)?
  • Draw the two camera icons on every later slide of the class.

3. Timeline Lab: Long Lines and Lightning

15–24 min
مختبر الخط الزمني: خطوط طويلة وبرق
Method: Timeline drawing (visual grammar) + meaning-contrast pairs
Purpose: See the shape of each form on a timeline and understand how one -ing changes the meaning.
Teacher says

“Draw a line: past on the left, now on the right. The background is a long wavy line: ~~~~~ I was studying. The event is a lightning bolt across it: the lights went out!”

“Now two actions in snapshots: When the bus arrived, we got on. Two dots: first the bus, then us.”

“Detective time! 'When Sara arrived, we ate.' 'When Sara arrived, we were eating.' Draw both. Who started eating first?”

“One -ing can change the whole story!”

  • الخلفية خط طويل متموّج، والحدث برق يقطعه.
  • When Sara arrived, we ate = وصلت ثم أكلنا. When Sara arrived, we were eating = كنا نأكل قبل وصولها.
Students do
  • Draw timelines for 3 sentences on paper and show them to the camera.
  • Answer each meaning question in the exercise.
  • Make one pair of their own: 'When Dad came home, I ___ / was ___ing.'
Exercise · meaning-detective — Read the sentence and answer the question about the timeline.
اقرأ الجملة وأجب عن سؤال الخط الزمني.
  1. When Sara arrived, we ate. Did we start eating before or after she arrived? → after
  2. When Sara arrived, we were eating. Did we start eating before or after she arrived? → before
  3. When the storm started, Omar ran to the shelter. Was Omar already running when the storm started? → no (he started running after the storm started)
  4. When the storm started, Omar was running to the shelter. Was Omar already running? → yes
  5. I was reviewing my notes when the teacher came in. Which action is longer? → reviewing my notes
  6. While we were exploring the old town, it started to rain. Which action interrupts? → it started to rain
  7. We packed our suitcases. Then we flew to Cairo. Background or a list of events? → a list of events (past simple, in order)
Tips:
  • Make students draw, not just answer: the drawing is the memory.
  • The pairs in items 1-4 are the heart of the class; spend time there.

4. Arabic Bridge: كان يلعب — Video or Habit?

24–32 min
جسر العربية: «كان يلعب» فيديو أم عادة؟
Method: Contrastive analysis + translation decisions
Purpose: Use the strong match كان + المضارع ≈ was/were + -ing for one moment in progress, and avoid the habit trap.
Teacher says

“Good news first: Arabic already has a video camera! كان يلعب (at one moment) = he was playing. كنت أدرس = I was studying. Two pieces in Arabic, two pieces in English.”

“Now a trap. 'كان يلعب كرة القدم كل يوم جمعة.' Is that one long action at one moment? No! It is a habit, many Fridays. English says: He played football every Friday, or He used to play football every Friday.”

“So before you translate كان + مضارع, ask: is it one moment in progress, or a habit? Moment = was playing. Habit = played / used to play.”

“And بينما? Usually while. عندما? when.”

  • العربية لديها كاميرا فيديو أيضاً: كان يلعب = was playing.
  • لكن «كان يلعب كل جمعة» عادة، وليست لحظة مستمرة؛ فنقول played أو used to play.
  • اسأل نفسك: لحظة مستمرة أم عادة؟
Students do
  • Decide 'moment' or 'habit' for each Arabic sentence, then translate it.
  • Explain one decision to the group.
  • Make one Arabic sentence of their own for a partner to translate.
Exercise · translation — Moment or habit? Translate into English.
لحظة أم عادة؟ ترجم إلى الإنجليزية.
  1. كنت أدرس عندما انقطعت الكهرباء. → Moment: I was studying when the lights (power) went out.
  2. كان جدي يزورنا كل أسبوع. → Habit: My grandpa visited us (used to visit us) every week.
  3. كانت تمطر عندما وصلنا إلى الفندق. → Moment: It was raining when we arrived at the hotel.
  4. بينما كان عمر ينتظر الباص، رأى صديقه. → Moment: While Omar was waiting for the bus, he saw his friend.
  5. كانت نور تتمرّن على البيانو كل يوم عندما كانت صغيرة. → Habit: Noor practiced (used to practice) the piano every day when she was little.
  6. كانوا يتحدثون عندما دخل المعلم. → Moment: They were talking when the teacher came in.
Tips:
  • For past habits, 'would' is also correct (My grandpa would visit us every week); accept it from strong students. Was + -ing for a habit is natural only in special cases (a short, temporary period, or a complaint with 'always'); keep the rule simple for this age.
  • Praise the Arabic knowledge: students who understand كان + مضارع already understand the past continuous.

5. Team Game: Interrupted!

32–41 min
لعبة الفرق: مقاطعة!
Method: Sentence combining race (gamified practice) with when / while
Purpose: Build accurate complex sentences with when and while, choosing the right form for each action.
Teacher says

“Two teams. I give you two actions: one long, one short. Join them with when or while. Correct sentence = 1 point. Correct in TWO ways (with when AND with while) = 2 points!”

“Example: I / sleep (long) + the thunder / wake me up (short). When: 'I was sleeping when the thunder woke me up.' While: 'While I was sleeping, the thunder woke me up.'”

“Ready? Interrupted!”

  • أعطيكم فعلين: طويلاً وقصيراً. اربطوهما بـ when أو while. جملة صحيحة = نقطة، وبطريقتين = نقطتان!
Students do
  • Combine the two actions orally, then type the sentence in the chat.
  • Try for the double-point version with both when and while.
  • Check the other team's sentence: is the long action in was/were + -ing?
Exercise · sentence-combining — Join the long action and the short action with when or while.
اربط الفعل الطويل بالفعل القصير باستخدام when أو while.
  1. we / wait at the bus stop + the storm / start → We were waiting at the bus stop when the storm started. / While we were waiting at the bus stop, the storm started.
  2. Hamad / pack his suitcase + his phone / ring → Hamad was packing his suitcase when his phone rang. / While Hamad was packing his suitcase, his phone rang.
  3. I / review my notes + I / find an old photo → I was reviewing my notes when I found an old photo. / While I was reviewing my notes, I found an old photo.
  4. they / explore the landmark + they / meet a famous actor → They were exploring the landmark when they met a famous actor. / While they were exploring the landmark, they met a famous actor.
  5. Mom / cook dinner + the lights / go out → Mom was cooking dinner when the lights went out. / While Mom was cooking dinner, the lights went out.
  6. Noor / walk to class + she / drop her umbrella → Noor was walking to class when she dropped her umbrella. / While Noor was walking to class, she dropped her umbrella.
  7. we / fly to Dubai + the pilot / make an announcement → We were flying to Dubai when the pilot made an announcement. / While we were flying to Dubai, the pilot made an announcement.
  8. Ziad / study for the exam + his friends / call him → Ziad was studying for the exam when his friends called him. / While Ziad was studying..., his friends called him.
Tips:
  • Accept both orders; point out the comma when the while/when part comes first.
  • If a team writes 'was rang', ask: is ringing long or short? Snapshot verbs stay simple.

6. Error Clinic: Wrong Camera!

41–47 min
عيادة الأخطاء: كاميرا خاطئة!
Method: Error correction with reasons (metalinguistic talk)
Purpose: Repair the typical errors: past simple for background, missing pieces, habits and state verbs.
Teacher says

“These sentences used the wrong camera, or lost a piece! Fix each one and say 'wrong camera', 'missing piece' or 'state verb'.”

  • هذه الجمل استخدمت الكاميرا الخاطئة أو فقدت قطعة! أصلح كل جملة واذكر السبب.
Students do
  • Correct the sentences in pairs.
  • Name the reason for each correction.
Exercise · error-correction — Correct the sentence and name the problem.
صحّح الجملة واذكر المشكلة.
  1. When I watched TV, the phone rang. (I was in the middle of a movie.) → When I was watching TV, the phone rang. (wrong camera: background needs -ing)
  2. I was study when the lights went out. → I was studying... (missing piece: -ing)
  3. They waiting at the airport when we arrived. → They were waiting... (missing piece: were)
  4. When I was ten, I was going to the beach every weekend. → I went / used to go to the beach every weekend. (habit, not a moment)
  5. I was knowing the answer, but I was nervous. → I knew the answer... (state verb)
  6. While the bus arrived, we were talking. → When the bus arrived, we were talking. / While we were talking, the bus arrived. (while + long action)
Tips:
  • Keep it quick; the reason words become class language for later lessons.
  • Lesson 16 reviews 'while' with two long actions; this is the foundation.

7. Director's Cut: Set the Scene, Then Action!

47–55 min
نسخة المخرج: اصنع المشهد ثم... أكشن!
Method: Creative writing in role (film director) + peer feedback
Purpose: Write a story opening with a background layer, a feeling, and an event that breaks in.
Teacher says

“You are film directors. Choose a scene. First, the video camera: two background sentences (weather, people, what was happening). Then a feeling: I was nervous / thrilled. Then... ACTION! One event with 'suddenly' or 'when'.”

“Example: 'It was raining hard. People were running to the shelter. I was waiting for the bus and I was very calm. Suddenly, there was a huge clap of thunder and...'”

“Read your scene with a director's voice. Listeners: say which sentence was the video and which was the click.”

  • أنتم مخرجون: جملتان للخلفية بكاميرا الفيديو، ثم شعور، ثم حدث مفاجئ مع suddenly أو when.
Students do
  • Choose one scene card and write 4-5 sentences (background, feeling, event).
  • Read the scene aloud dramatically.
  • Peer feedback: find the video sentences and the click sentence; give one star and one wish.
Exercise · creative-writing — Choose a scene. Write 2 background sentences, 1 feeling, 1 event.
اختر مشهداً. اكتب جملتين للخلفية وشعوراً واحداً وحدثاً واحداً.
  1. Scene 1: the bus stop in a storm → Model: It was raining and the wind was blowing. Three people were waiting under one umbrella. I was cold but calm. Suddenly, a car stopped and my uncle opened the door!
  2. Scene 2: the exam room → Model: It was very quiet. Everybody was writing. I was nervous. When I turned the page, I saw a question I knew!
  3. Scene 3: the airport → Model: The airport was crowded. Families were pushing big suitcases. I was thrilled. Suddenly, they called my name!
  4. Scene 4: the hotel at night → Model: It was midnight. My brother was sleeping and I was reading. Suddenly, someone knocked on the door.
  5. Scene 5: the school trip to a landmark → Model: The sun was shining. Our teacher was explaining the history of the old castle. I was looking at the old walls when I saw a small door.
  6. Scene 6: the football match → Model: Thousands of fans were singing. We were losing 1-0. I was disappointed. Then, in the last minute, Omar scored!
Tips:
  • Models are examples only; any scene with correct background and event forms is right.
  • Ask students to save their scenes: Bonus Class 4 turns them into dramatic readings.

8. Exit Ticket, Did You Know? and Goodbye

55–60 min
بطاقة الخروج وهل تعلم؟ والوداع
Method: Formative check + curiosity close
Purpose: Check form, meaning and the Arabic trap individually.
Teacher says

“Exit ticket: five questions in the chat.”

“Did you know? Film scripts use the same two layers! A script describes the scene first ('Rain is falling. People are waiting.') and then the action. Storytelling and film-making share one grammar of attention.”

“Next class: we learn to SAY our stories like real storytellers. Bring your scene!”

  • هل تعلم؟ نصوص الأفلام تستخدم الطبقتين نفسيهما: وصف المشهد أولاً ثم الحدث.
Students do
  • Answer the 5 exit-ticket items.
  • Say goodbye with 'Yesterday at this time I was...'
Tips:
  • Students under 4/5 need more timeline drawing in the Lesson 13-16 warm-ups.

Exit ticket

Exercise · exit ticket — Answer quickly.
أجب بسرعة.
  1. Complete: I ___ (sleep) when the thunder started. → was sleeping
  2. Complete: While we were waiting for the bus, it ___ (start) to rain. → started
  3. 'When Dad arrived, we were eating.' Did we start eating before or after he arrived? → before
  4. Translate: كان يلعب التنس كل صباح. → He played (used to play) tennis every morning. (habit)
  5. Correct it: I was knowing his name. → I knew his name.

Homework

Two-Layer Story: write the opening of a story (80-100 words) about a trip or a stormy day. Use at least three was/were + -ing sentences for the background, one feeling from Lesson 9, and three events in the past simple with when, while and suddenly. Draw the timeline under your story.

قصة الطبقتين: اكتب بداية قصة (80-100 كلمة) عن رحلة أو يوم عاصف. استخدم ثلاث جمل على الأقل بـ was/were + -ing للخلفية، وشعوراً واحداً من الدرس 9، وثلاثة أحداث بالماضي البسيط مع when و while و suddenly. ارسم الخط الزمني تحت قصتك.

  • Write the two-layer story opening (80-100 words).
  • Underline background verbs in one color and event verbs in another.
  • Draw the timeline with wavy lines and lightning bolts.

Support

  • Give the two-camera cards with the forms printed on them: video = was/were + -ing; photo = -ed / fossil.
  • In the Interrupted! game, mark which action is long before students combine.
  • Let the student say the story in Arabic first, then choose the camera for each verb.

Challenge

  • Use two background actions at the same time: While Mom was cooking, Dad was cleaning the car.
  • Write the same mini-story twice, once with 'we ate' and once with 'we were eating', and explain how the story changes.
  • Find a past continuous sentence in a song, movie or book and explain its job.

Success criteria

  • Classifies at least 6 of 8 story sentences as background or event.
  • Answers the meaning pairs (we ate / we were eating) correctly and draws the timelines.
  • Combines at least 6 of 8 action pairs correctly with when / while.
  • Translates كان + المضارع correctly as moment or habit in at least 4 of 6 items.

Watch for

  • Past simple for background ('When I watched TV, the phone rang')
  • 'I was study' / 'They waiting'
  • Past continuous for habits
  • State verbs with -ing
  • while + a short action

Teacher notes

  • The core message is meaning, not form: the form was taught in Lesson 10. Keep asking 'video or photo?'.
  • The habit trap (كان يلعب كل يوم) is the most important Arabic point of the class; give it time.
  • Be modest about the Arabic aspect claim: 'many linguists describe it this way' is accurate; 'Arabic has no tense' is not.
  • Bonus Class 4 uses the scenes written today for dramatic reading with stress and rhythm.
