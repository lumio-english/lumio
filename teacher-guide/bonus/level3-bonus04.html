Time Inside Verbs: Simple vs Continuous

الزمن داخل الأفعال: البسيط والمستمر
Big idea: English uses two presents: the simple for habits and facts (I study every day) and the continuous for things in progress now (I am studying right now). Arabic يدرس can mean both, and state verbs like know and want stay simple.
في الإنجليزية مضارعان: البسيط للعادات والحقائق (I study every day)، والمستمر لما يحدث الآن (I am studying right now). أما الفعل العربي يدرس فقد يحمل المعنيين، وأفعال الحالة مثل know و want تبقى في صيغة البسيط.

Linguistics Corner — Tense and aspect: two kinds of time · الزمن والجهة: نوعان من الوقت

Linguists separate tense (when: past or present) from aspect (how we see the action: a complete habit or something in progress). I play and I am playing are both present tense; they differ in aspect.يفرّق اللغويون بين الزمن (متى: ماضٍ أو حاضر) والجهة (كيف ننظر إلى الحدث: عادة كاملة أم شيء جارٍ الآن). I play و I am playing كلاهما في الزمن الحاضر، والفرق بينهما في الجهة.
Many languages build their 'in progress' form from words for place or posture. Dutch says 'Ik ben aan het lezen' (I am at the reading). Spanish 'estoy leyendo' uses estar, from a Latin verb meaning 'to stand'. Gulf Arabic says قاعد يدرس, literally 'sitting studying'.كثير من اللغات تبني صيغة 'الحدث الجاري' من كلمات تدل على المكان أو هيئة الجسم. الهولندية تقول 'Ik ben aan het lezen' أي 'أنا عند القراءة'. والإسبانية 'estoy leyendo' تستعمل estar المأخوذ من فعل لاتيني معناه 'يقف'. وفي اللهجة الخليجية يقال 'قاعد يدرس' أي حرفياً 'جالس يدرس'.
One theory says English did something similar: 'He is on hunting' became 'He is a-hunting' (you still hear it in old songs) and then 'He is hunting'.ترى إحدى النظريات أن الإنجليزية فعلت شيئاً مشابهاً: 'He is on hunting' صارت 'He is a-hunting' (ما زالت تُسمع في الأغاني القديمة) ثم 'He is hunting'.
Many languages, including standard Arabic, French and German, use one present form for both habits and actions in progress. English is one of the languages that must choose every time you speak in the present.لغات كثيرة، منها العربية الفصحى والفرنسية والألمانية، تستعمل صيغة مضارع واحدة للعادة وللحدث الجاري. أما الإنجليزية فمن اللغات التي يجب أن تختار في كل مرة تتكلم فيها عن الحاضر.

Key terms

TermبالعربيةMeaning
present simpleالمضارع البسيطHabits, routines and facts: I study after dinner. She streams every weekend.
present continuousالمضارع المستمرam / is / are + verb-ing for an action in progress now: I am studying right now.
tenseالزمنWHEN something happens: past or present.
aspectالجهة (هيئة الحدث)HOW we look at the action: as a whole habit or fact, or as something still in progress.
state verb (stative verb)فعل حالةA verb for a state, not an action: know, like, love, want, need, belong, understand, have (own). Normally not used in -ing form.
signal wordsكلمات دالّةTime words that point to the form: every day, usually, on Fridays (simple) / now, right now, at the moment, Look! (continuous).

Common errors of Arabic speakers

Student saysCorrectWhy
I am knowing the answer.I know the answer.Know is a state verb. Students who just learned -ing add it to every present verb.
She studying for a quiz.She is studying for a quiz.Missing be, as in هي تدرس (no verb 'to be' in Arabic). The continuous needs am / is / are.
I am go to school every day.I go to school every day.A habit needs the simple form. 'I am' is not a general 'present' marker.
Look! Ziad plays the game.Look! Ziad is playing the game.Arabic يلعب covers 'now', so students use the simple. Look! means it is happening at this moment.
I am having a new controller.I have a new controller.Have for owning is a state. (But 'I am having lunch' is fine: there have means eat, an action.)
We are play a multiplayer game.We are playing a multiplayer game.The -ing is missing. The continuous always needs both parts: are + playing.

1. Warm-up: Lumi Says ... What Are You Doing?

0–6 min
إحماء: لومي يقول ... ماذا تفعل؟
Method: TPR with imperatives (Lesson 13) leading into present continuous description
Purpose: Revise imperatives and produce live present continuous sentences about real actions on camera.
Teacher says

“Lumi says: raise your hand! ... Freeze! Noor, what are you doing? ... 'I am raising my hand!'”

“Lumi says: touch your head! ... Omar, what are you doing?”

“Pay attention! (no 'Lumi says') ... Oh, Ziad moved! Out for one round!”

“Now the twist: Sara, what do you do every evening? ... and what are you doing now? Two different questions!”

  • عندما أقول Freeze توقف، وقل لي ماذا تفعل الآن.
  • لاحظوا: سؤالان مختلفان: ماذا تفعل كل مساء؟ وماذا تفعل الآن؟
Students do
  • Follow the commands on camera (raise your hand, touch your head, hold up a pen, close your eyes, look at the camera).
  • Freeze and describe the action: I am raising my hand.
  • Answer two questions: What do you do every evening? / What are you doing now?
Tips:
  • Keep commands short and physical; everyone moves.
  • The two final questions are the hook: write both answers on the slide for the next stage.

2. Discovery: Routine or Snapshot?

6–18 min
اكتشاف: عادة أم لقطة؟
Method: Guided discovery with timelines and signal words (find-the-rule)
Purpose: Students discover the meaning difference (habit vs in progress), the form (am/is/are + -ing) and the signal words.
Teacher says

“Two pictures of Sara. Picture 1: a calendar, Sara streaming every Saturday. 'Sara streams every Saturday.' Picture 2: a camera photo, Sara with her headset on. 'Sara is streaming right now.'”

“Which one is a ROUTINE - it happens again and again? ... 1! Which one is a SNAPSHOT - it is happening at this moment? ... 2!”

“Look at the form. Routine: streams. Snapshot: is streaming. How many parts does the snapshot verb have? ... Two! Helper + -ing.”

“Now find the signal words. Routine friends: every day, usually, always, on Fridays, after school. Snapshot friends: now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!”

“Rule time: write your own rule for routine and snapshot.”

  • العادة تتكرر مرة بعد مرة، واللقطة تحدث في هذه اللحظة.
  • اللقطة تحتاج إلى جزأين: am / is / are + الفعل مع -ing.
  • ابحثوا عن الكلمات الدالّة: every day للعادة، و right now للّقطة.
Students do
  • Match each picture to its sentence.
  • Count the parts of the continuous verb and colour the helper yellow and the -ing verb red.
  • Sort signal words into two columns.
  • Hold up ROUTINE (calendar) or SNAPSHOT (camera) for each sentence in the exercise.
Exercise · routine-or-snapshot — Routine or snapshot? Show the card and say the signal word.
عادة أم لقطة؟ ارفع البطاقة واذكر الكلمة الدالّة.
  1. Hamad trains with his team every Tuesday. → ROUTINE (every Tuesday)
  2. Look! Omar is scrolling his phone in class. → SNAPSHOT (Look!)
  3. We review our notes before every quiz. → ROUTINE (before every quiz)
  4. Noor is highlighting the key points right now. → SNAPSHOT (right now)
  5. My cousins usually play multiplayer games at night. → ROUTINE (usually)
  6. Shh! The baby is sleeping. → SNAPSHOT (Shh! = it is happening now)
  7. Ziad always pauses the game to get snacks. → ROUTINE (always)
  8. At the moment, they are working on a group project. → SNAPSHOT (at the moment)
Tips:
  • Use the camera/calendar images every time; they are easier to remember than the terms.
  • Item 6 has no classic signal word: the situation tells you. Good thinking practice.
  • Spelling reminder if needed: study -> studying, chill -> chilling, write -> writing.

3. Linguistics Corner: Tense, Aspect and 'Sitting Studying'

18–25 min
ركن اللغويات: الزمن والجهة و'قاعد يدرس'
Method: Mini-lecture with cross-language examples + quick quiz
Purpose: Broaden general knowledge: the difference between tense and aspect, and how languages build progressive forms.
Teacher says

“Linguists talk about two kinds of time. Tense: WHEN - past or present. Aspect: HOW you see the action - a whole habit, or a scene in progress.”

“I play and I am playing: same tense (present), different aspect.”

“Cool fact: many languages build the 'in progress' form from place or posture words. Dutch: 'I am at the reading.' Spanish uses estar, from a Latin verb meaning 'to stand'. And in the Gulf? قاعد يدرس - literally 'sitting studying'!”

“Standard Arabic, French and German use one present for both. English makes you choose every time. That's why it feels hard - and why you're now a real grammar scientist.”

  • الزمن: متى؟ والجهة: كيف ننظر إلى الحدث؟
  • لغات كثيرة تبني صيغة 'الحدث الجاري' من كلمات المكان أو الجلوس والوقوف، مثل 'قاعد يدرس' في الخليج.
Students do
  • Answer: same tense or different tense? same aspect or different aspect?
  • Share how they say 'I'm playing now' in their own Arabic dialect.
Exercise · tense-or-aspect — What is different in each pair: tense (when) or aspect (how we see it)?
ما الذي يختلف في كل زوج: الزمن (متى) أم الجهة (كيف نراه)؟
  1. I play. / I am playing. → aspect
  2. She studies. / She is studying. → aspect
  3. I play. / I played. → tense
  4. We chat. / We are chatting. → aspect
  5. He wins. / He won. → tense
  6. They review. / They are reviewing. → aspect
Tips:
  • Students met won in Lesson 11; the past items are only for contrast, not for teaching the past tense.
  • The a-hunting history is a theory; mention it only if time allows and present it as 'one idea linguists have'.
  • Dialects differ: invite students to share their own forms (قاعد، عم يدرس in Levantine, بيدرس in Egyptian) without judging any as better.

4. Arabic Bridge: One Arabic Verb, Two English Verbs

25–33 min
جسر العربية: فعل عربي واحد وفعلان إنجليزيان
Method: Contrastive analysis + translation with context
Purpose: Show that أدرس / يلعب need context to choose the English form, and fix the missing-be error.
Teacher says

“أدرس كل يوم and أدرس الآن. Same Arabic verb! The time word does the work.”

“English is different: the verb itself changes. I study every day. I am studying now.”

“Spoken Arabic sometimes helps: in the Gulf, قاعد ألعب = I am playing. In Egypt, بالعب works for both, so you must think!”

“And watch out: هي تدرس has no 'is'. English continuous needs it: She IS studying. Two parts, always.”

“One Arabic question, ماذا تفعل؟, becomes two English questions: What do you do? (habits) What are you doing? (now).”

  • الفعل العربي واحد، وكلمة الزمن تحدد المعنى. في الإنجليزية يتغير الفعل نفسه.
  • لا تنسَ is: She is studying، جزآن دائماً.
Students do
  • Translate Arabic sentences, choosing simple or continuous from the time words.
  • Explain the clue word for each choice.
  • Ask and answer: What do you do after school? / What are you doing now?
Exercise · translate-with-context — Say it in English. Is it a routine or a snapshot?
قلها بالإنجليزية. هل هي عادة أم لقطة؟
  1. أدرس كل يوم بعد العشاء. → I study every day after dinner.
  2. أدرس الآن. → I am studying now.
  3. هي تلعب ألعاب الفيديو الآن. → She is playing video games now.
  4. يلعب كرة السلة كل أسبوع. → He plays basketball every week.
  5. نحن نراجع ملاحظاتنا الآن. → We are reviewing our notes now.
  6. ماذا تفعل الآن؟ → What are you doing now?
  7. ماذا تفعل في عطلة نهاية الأسبوع عادةً؟ → What do you usually do at the weekend?
  8. أعرف الإجابة. → I know the answer. (state verb, no -ing)
Tips:
  • If a student says 'She studying', point back to Bonus Class 1: 'Where is the red word? English needs is.'
  • Accept 'on the weekend' (American) and 'at the weekend' (British).

5. State Verb Lab: The No -ing Zone

33–43 min
مختبر أفعال الحالة: منطقة ممنوع فيها -ing
Method: Inductive discovery (sorting action vs state) + choice practice
Purpose: Discover that state verbs (know, like, want, need, belong, understand, have for owning) are normally not continuous.
Teacher says

“Experiment! Can you see a camera photo of 'knowing'? Can you take a snapshot of 'wanting'? ... Not really! They're not actions - they're states. They're in your head or your heart, not in your hands.”

“Sort these verbs: play, know, run, want, text, like, stream, need, belong, highlight. Action or state?”

“Rule: state verbs live in the No -ing Zone. I know, I want, I like - even right now!”

“Tricky one: have. 'I have a new controller' - owning, a state, no -ing. 'I am having lunch' - eating, an action, -ing is fine!”

“Real-life note: you may hear the advert 'I'm lovin' it'. That's advertising breaking the rule on purpose to sound fun.”

  • أفعال الحالة ليست أحداثاً تُصوَّر، بل حالات في العقل أو القلب، لذلك لا نضيف لها -ing عادةً حتى لو كانت الآن.
  • have بمعنى يملك: بلا -ing. have بمعنى يأكل: يمكن أن نقول I am having lunch.
Students do
  • Sort 10 verbs into ACTION and STATE (can you take a photo of it?).
  • Choose the correct form in 10 sentences.
  • Explain the 'have' difference with their own example.
Exercise · choose-the-form — Choose the correct form. Watch for state verbs!
اختر الصيغة الصحيحة، وانتبه لأفعال الحالة!
  1. I (know / am knowing) the password. → know
  2. Look! Hamad (levels up / is leveling up)! → is leveling up
  3. We (need / are needing) a better strategy. → need
  4. This controller (belongs / is belonging) to Ziad. → belongs
  5. Shh! Noor (memorizes / is memorizing) her presentation. → is memorizing
  6. I (have / am having) a new avatar. → have
  7. They (have / are having) lunch in the cafeteria right now. → are having
  8. Sara (wants / is wanting) the high score. → wants
  9. I (don't understand / am not understanding) this riddle. → don't understand
  10. My teammates (like / are liking) the new game. → like
Tips:
  • The photo test (can you take a picture of it?) works for most verbs and is easy to remember.
  • 'Levelling' is British spelling; 'leveling' is American. Accept both.
  • Do not overload: think (opinion vs process) is a challenge item only.

6. Team Challenge: Lumi's Live Stream

43–54 min
تحدي الفرق: البث المباشر مع لومي
Method: Task-based gap-fill story + streamer role-play (team competition)
Purpose: Use both presents and state verbs together in a connected text, then in free speaking.
Teacher says

“Lumi is live-streaming Game Night! Read the chat. Each team fills the gaps. 2 points per correct gap, 1 bonus point if you name the clue.”

“Part 2: you are the streamer! Hold something - a pen, a controller, a book - and do an action on camera. Commentate: 'Hi followers! Right now I am... Usually I... but today...'”

“Each streamer gets 30 seconds. The other team counts correct verbs: one point each.”

  • املؤوا الفراغات في محادثة البث، واذكروا الكلمة الدالّة لتحصلوا على نقطة إضافية.
  • الآن أنت صانع المحتوى! صف ما تفعله الآن وما تفعله عادةً.
Students do
  • Fill the gaps in the Game Night chat in teams and explain each choice.
  • Do a 30-second live-stream commentary on camera using at least two continuous and two simple sentences.
  • Listen to the other team and count their correct verbs.
Exercise · gap-fill-story — Lumi's Game Night chat: put the verb in the present simple or continuous.
محادثة ليلة الألعاب: ضع الفعل في المضارع البسيط أو المستمر.
  1. Hi crew! Right now I ___ (play) a multiplayer game with my teammates. → am playing
  2. We usually ___ (play) on Fridays, but tonight is special. → play
  3. Hamad ___ (not / play) with us now. → isn't playing
  4. He ___ (study) for tomorrow's quiz at the moment. → is studying
  5. He ___ (want) a high score in science! → wants
  6. Ziad ___ (know) the best strategy for this level. → knows
  7. Look! Sara ___ (stream) our game live! → is streaming
  8. She ___ (stream) every weekend and she ___ (have) 500 followers. → streams / has
  9. Wait... why ___ you ___ (pause) the game, Omar? → are ... pausing
  10. ___ you ___ (need) a new controller? → Do ... need
Tips:
  • Items 9 and 10 combine Bonus Class 3 (questions) with today's point: helper + subject + verb.
  • In the streamer part, praise natural mixing: 'Right now I'm drawing, but usually I draw on Fridays.'
  • With 1-2 students: the teacher streams first as a model, deliberately making 2 mistakes for the students to catch.

7. Exit Ticket, Did You Know? and Mission

54–60 min
بطاقة الخروج وهل تعلم؟ والمهمة
Method: Formative check + Grammar Lab graduation close
Purpose: Check the simple/continuous choice individually and close the Level 3 Grammar Lab series.
Teacher says

“Exit ticket: five items, alone, private chat.”

“Did you know? English is one of the few languages where 'Do you play football?' and 'Are you playing football?' ask totally different things. Now you can hear the difference!”

“Grammar Lab graduates! In four classes you learned word jobs, word order, the Question Machine and time inside verbs. That's how English sentences work.”

“Your mission: tonight, at 3 different times, text or say to someone at home what you are doing right now - in English!”

  • هل تعلم؟ في الإنجليزية Do you play football? و Are you playing football? سؤالان مختلفان تماماً، وأنت الآن تسمع الفرق!
  • تهانينا يا خرّيجي مختبر القواعد!
Students do
  • Answer 5 items in a private chat.
  • Say one routine and one snapshot sentence about themselves to close.
Tips:
  • Note who still uses -ing with state verbs; add a 'No -ing Zone' warm-up in Lessons 17-19.
  • Lesson 20 is the Big Review: reuse the four Bonus Class colour codes and the Question Machine there.

Exit ticket

Exercise · exit ticket — Write the correct form or fix the sentence.
اكتب الصيغة الصحيحة أو أصلح الجملة.
  1. Sara ___ (text) her cousin every evening. → texts
  2. Listen! Somebody ___ (knock) on the door. → is knocking
  3. Fix it: I am knowing the answer. → I know the answer.
  4. Fix it: Omar studying in the library now. → Omar is studying in the library now.
  5. Ask about now: (you / do) → What are you doing?

Homework

Routine vs Snapshot Diary: write 4 sentences about your usual evening (simple) and take 4 'snapshots' tonight - write what you and your family are doing at 4 different moments (continuous). Use one state verb in each part.

يوميات العادة واللقطة: اكتب 4 جمل عن مسائك المعتاد (بسيط) والتقط 4 'لقطات' الليلة: اكتب ماذا تفعل أنت وأسرتك في 4 لحظات مختلفة (مستمر). استعمل فعل حالة واحداً في كل جزء.

  • Write 4 routine sentences with signal words (usually, every day...).
  • Write 4 snapshot sentences with the time (At 7:30, my brother is gaming...).
  • Record a 30-second 'live stream' voice note describing what you are doing.

Support

  • Use only I and he/she in all tasks; add we/they later.
  • Give the signal word in bold in every exercise item.
  • Show the two-part continuous frame (am/is/are + -ing) on screen all class.

Challenge

  • Think: 'I think it's a good game' (opinion, state) vs 'I'm thinking about the answer' (process, action). Find two more verbs that change meaning like this (have, see).
  • Present continuous for future plans: 'I'm playing football tomorrow.' Spot the time word that changes the meaning.
  • Write a 6-line sports commentary using only the present continuous, then add 2 background facts in the simple.

Success criteria

  • Sorts at least 7 of 8 sentences as routine or snapshot with the signal word.
  • Uses am / is / are + -ing with both parts in speaking and writing.
  • Chooses the simple with state verbs in at least 8 of 10 items.
  • Uses both presents correctly in a 30-second commentary.

Watch for

  • -ing with state verbs: I am knowing / I am wanting / I am having a phone
  • Missing be: She studying
  • Missing -ing: We are play
  • Continuous for habits: I am go to school every day
  • Simple for actions in progress after Look! / Listen! / right now

Teacher notes

  • This is the last Grammar Lab class of Level 3. Briefly connect all four: word jobs (B1), word order (B2), questions (B3) and verb time (B4).
  • Present the state-verb rule as 'normally' not 'never': modern English sometimes bends it (I'm loving it), and some verbs change meaning (think, have). Keep the rule strong for production at this level.
  • Regional Arabic forms differ: Gulf قاعد, Levantine عم, Egyptian بـ. Ask students for their own forms; never present one dialect as correct.
