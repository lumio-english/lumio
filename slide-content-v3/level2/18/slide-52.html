SENTENCE PATTERNS

This/That/These/Those + noun
“This is my pencil.”
“That is your bag.”
“These are my books.”
“Those are your shoes.”
“Come here, please.”
“The ball is over there.”
“This is my pencil. That is your bag.”
“These are my books. Those are yours.”
“Come here! The ball is over there.”

GRAMMAR

This / That / These / Those
“This is my book. (near, one)”
“That is your book. (far, one)”
“These are my pens. (near, many)”
“Those are your pens. (far, many)”

DIALOGUE

“This is my pencil. What is that?”
“That is your bag, over there.”
