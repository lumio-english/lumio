SENTENCE PATTERNS (cont.)

“A month has thirty days.”
“A year has twelve months.”
“January is the first month.”
“June is warm. September is back to school.”
“December is a cool month.”

DIALOGUE

“When is your birthday?”
“My birthday is in January. What month is it now?”
“It is September. School just started.”
“My favorite month is June, no school!”
