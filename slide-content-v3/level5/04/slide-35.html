THE PATTERN
Was / Were
was / were
"Was" is the past form of "is/am" (I, he, she, it). "Were" is the past form of "are" (you, we, they).
"Was" هي صيغة الماضي لـ "is/am". "Were" هي صيغة الماضي لـ "are".
