Will (Future Predictions) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I will to call you later.
I will call you later.
No "to" after will.
لا نضع "to" بعد will.
2
She wills help us.
She will help us.
Will never changes.
will لا تتغير أبداً.
3
It will rains tomorrow.
It will rain tomorrow.
After will, the verb has no -s.
بعد will لا يأخذ الفعل -s.
