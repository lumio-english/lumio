Going to (Future Plans) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I going to visit my uncle.
I am going to visit my uncle.
Going to needs am/is/are.
going to تحتاج am/is/are.
2
She is going to visits Dubai.
She is going to visit Dubai.
After going to, use the base verb.
بعد going to نستخدم الفعل الأساسي.
3
We are go to watch a film tonight.
We are going to watch a film tonight.
It's "going to", with -ing.
الصيغة هي "going to" مع -ing.
