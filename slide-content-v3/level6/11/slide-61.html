“Do you usually speak confidently in front of people, or does it make you nervous?”
“Honestly, nervously at first, but I try to speak clearly no matter how I feel.”
“That's smart. Do you think it's better to speak politely or just be blunt?”
“Politely, but still honestly. You can be kind and truthful at the same time.”
“That's a good balance, actually. I struggle with saying things too bluntly sometimes.”
“Practice helps. The more confidently you speak, the more naturally kindness comes through.”
