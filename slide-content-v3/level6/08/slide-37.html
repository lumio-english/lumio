THE PATTERN
Going to (Future Plans)
going to (خطط المستقبل)
We use "am/is/are + going to + verb" to talk about plans and intentions for the future.
نستخدم "am/is/are + going to + الفعل" للحديث عن خطط ونوايا للمستقبل.
