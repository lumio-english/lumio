Comparatives & Superlatives with Long Adjectives · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
This film is interestinger than that one.
This film is more interesting than that one.
Long adjectives use "more".
الصفات الطويلة تستخدم "more".
2
She is the most happiest girl.
She is the happiest girl.
Don't use "most" AND -est together.
لا نستخدم "most" و -est معاً.
3
Math is more difficult that art.
Math is more difficult than art.
Comparatives use "than".
المقارنة تستخدم "than".
