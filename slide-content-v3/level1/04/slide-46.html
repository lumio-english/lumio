SENTENCE PATTERNS

big / small with It is...
“It is big.”
“It is small.”
“The elephant is big.”
“The mouse is small.”
“The ball is big.”
“The box is small.”
“The elephant is big!”
“The mouse is small!”
“The ball is big. The box is small.”

GRAMMAR

What is it? / Is it...? Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
“What is it? It is an elephant.”
“Is it big? Yes, it is.”
“Is it small? No, it isn't.”
“What is it? It is a mouse. It is small.”

DIALOGUE

“Look at that elephant!”
“It is big! And look at the mouse.”
