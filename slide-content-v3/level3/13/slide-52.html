Imperatives (Commands) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
You open the window, please.
Open the window, please.
Commands start with the verb -- no subject.
الأوامر تبدأ بالفعل — بدون فاعل.
2
Don't to touch that!
Don't touch that!
Don't + base verb, no "to".
Don't + الفعل الأساسي، بدون "to".
3
Please sits down.
Please sit down.
The command verb never takes -s.
فعل الأمر لا يأخذ -s أبداً.
