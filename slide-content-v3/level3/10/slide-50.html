Wh- Questions · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
Where you live?
Where do you live?
Wh- word + do/does + subject + verb.
كلمة الاستفهام + do/does + الفاعل + الفعل.
2
What time does she goes to bed?
What time does she go to bed?
After "does" the verb is the base form.
بعد "does" يأتي الفعل في صيغته الأساسية.
3
Why he is late?
Why is he late?
With "be", the verb comes before the subject.
مع فعل "be" يأتي الفعل قبل الفاعل.
