Comparatives (bigger, smaller) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
My bag is more big than yours.
My bag is bigger than yours.
Short adjectives take -er, not "more".
الصفات القصيرة تأخذ -er وليس "more".
2
This book is gooder than that one.
This book is better than that one.
Good is irregular: better.
good صفة شاذة: better.
3
She is taller that me.
She is taller than me.
Comparatives use "than".
المقارنة تستخدم "than".
