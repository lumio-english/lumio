Prepositions of Place · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
The cat is in the table.
The cat is on the table.
On a surface → on. Inside something → in.
على سطح ← on. داخل شيء ← in.
2
I sit next my friend.
I sit next to my friend.
It's always "next to".
نقول دائماً "next to".
3
The ball is under of the bed.
The ball is under the bed.
Under has no "of".
under لا تأتي مع "of".
