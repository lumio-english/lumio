Present Continuous (I am doing) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I eating lunch now.
I am eating lunch now.
Present Continuous needs am/is/are.
المضارع المستمر يحتاج am/is/are.
2
She is study for the test.
She is studying for the test.
The verb needs -ing.
الفعل يحتاج -ing.
3
They are play football right now.
They are playing football right now.
are + verb-ing.
are + الفعل مع -ing.
