Want to + verb (future goals) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
I want achieve my goals.
I want to achieve my goals.
want + to + verb.
بعد want نضع to ثم الفعل.
2
She want to be a doctor.
She wants to be a doctor.
She -> wants.
مع she نقول wants.
3
I want to learning coding.
I want to learn coding.
After "to" the verb is in its base form.
بعد to يأتي الفعل في صيغته الأساسية.
