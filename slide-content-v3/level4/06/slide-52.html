Possessive Pronouns (mine, yours, his, hers) · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
This phone is my.
This phone is mine.
At the end of a sentence use mine, not my.
في نهاية الجملة نستخدم mine وليس my.
2
Is this book your?
Is this book yours?
No noun after it → yours.
لا يوجد اسم بعدها ← yours.
3
The red bag is her.
The red bag is hers.
Hers stands alone; her needs a noun.
hers تأتي وحدها، أما her فتحتاج اسماً بعدها.
