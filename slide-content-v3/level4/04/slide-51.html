Prepositions of Time · Find the mistake in each sentence, then tap to check.Pairs: who spots it first? · 1-on-1: say the correct sentence before you reveal it.
1
The class starts in 4 o'clock.
The class starts at 4 o'clock.
Exact times → at.
الوقت المحدد ← at.
2
My birthday is on March.
My birthday is in March.
Months → in.
الشهور ← in.
3
We play football in Friday.
We play football on Friday.
Days → on.
الأيام ← on.
