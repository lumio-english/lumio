Real writing practice. Real flashcards. Off the screen.

Every single lesson, at every level, comes with two things you can print and hold: a homework sheet and a set of flashcards. Neither is an afterthought — both are written specifically for the age and stage your child is actually at.

Writing exercises

Who they're for: every level, but the task grows with your child. Pre-A and the early levels trace and copy each word by hand, building letter formation and muscle memory before reading is even required. From Level 3 onward, the task becomes writing full original sentences in that day's grammar pattern — putting the rule to use, not just recognizing it.

Why it matters: screen recognition and handwriting are different skills, and only one of them is practiced by tapping an answer. A short, guided writing task each lesson builds the one that a multiple-choice quiz can't.

Flashcards

Who they're for: every level, and every parent — including parents who don't speak English themselves. Each card shows the picture and English word on one side and the Arabic translation on the other, so quizzing at home never requires knowing the answer in advance.

Why it matters: spaced, repeated recall away from a screen is one of the most reliable ways vocabulary actually sticks. A five-minute flip-through before bed does real work that a single lesson session can't do alone.

Questions parents ask

What age is Lumio actually for?

From 4 all the way to 15 — the curriculum spans 7 levels, each built for a specific age range, from a child's very first English words through confident, independent conversation.

Is there a real teacher, or is this just an app?

Both. Your child prepares in the app, then joins a real, live class with their teacher over Zoom — the app isn't a replacement for the teacher, it's what makes that class time count.

My child has never studied English. Where do they start?

Take the free placement test — it's quick, and it places your child at the right level automatically based on their real score, whether that's their very first English words or something further along.

Can we try a class before paying anything?

Yes — pick your child's age group and join a free trial class right now, in the same format as a real Lumio lesson. No card, no signup required to try it.

Is the Arabic support just a translation button?

No — Arabic is built into every lesson from the start: every word, every instruction, every spelling rule has its Arabic alongside it, not a toggle bolted on afterward.

What happens after class ends?

Homework, right away — real sentences to practice, words to spell, and a quick quiz, all tied to exactly what was covered in that day's class.
